## Intro

Fractions with same denominator, numerators can be added, even with rational expressions.

$$\frac{1}{x}+\frac{5}{x}=\frac{6}{x}$$

## Adding Rational Expressions With a Common Denominator

Simplify:

$$\frac{2+a}{2a}+\frac{3+2a}{2a}=\frac{5+3a}{2a}$$

If they do not have the same denominator, multiply to make them the same.

## Adding Rational Expressions in One Variable with Common Factors in the Denominators

$$\frac{3}{2x}+\frac{y}{x}=\frac{3}{2x}+\frac22\cdot\frac{y}{x}=\frac{3+2y}{2x}$$
