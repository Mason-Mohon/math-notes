Math Academy Algebra II Riemann Sum

Right riemann sum is very similar to [Approximating Areas With the Left Riemann Sum](Approximating%20Areas%20With%20the%20Left%20Riemann%20Sum.md) but instead the right corner of the box touches the curve.

## Question 1
Interval \[0, 2]

| x    | 0.5 | 1   | 1.5 | 2   |
| ---- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| f(x) | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   |

$$\displaylines{\Delta x=05\\ \\ y=2x+1\\ \\ A=14\cdot0.5=7}$$

## Question 5

![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260628141546.png)
5 subintervals
$$\displaylines{A=36,\Delta x=2\\ \\ 36=\left(k+6+5+4\right)\cdot2\\ \\ 18=k+15,k=3}$$

## Question 6
Interval \[-1, 0]
$$\displaylines{\Delta x=0.25\\ \\ y=\left(x+2\right)^3-1\\ \\ f\left(-0.75\right)=0.9531,f\left(-0.5\right)=2.375,f\left(-0.25\right)=4.359,f\left(0\right)=7\\ \\ A=\left(0.9531+2.375+4.359+7\right)\cdot0.25=3.67}$$

## Question 7
Interval \[1, 3]
$$\displaylines{\Delta x=0.5,y=10-2x\\ \\ f\left(1.5\right)=7,f\left(2\right)=6,f\left(2.5\right)=5,f\left(3\right)=4\\ \\ A=\left(7+6+5+4\right)\cdot0.5=11}$$
