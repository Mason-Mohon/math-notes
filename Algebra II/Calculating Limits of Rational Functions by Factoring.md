## Q1
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{x\rightarrow3}\dfrac{x-3}{3x^2-5x-12}}=}\frac{x-3}{\left(x-3\right)\left(3x+4\right)}=\frac{1}{3x+4}=\frac{1}{13}$$
