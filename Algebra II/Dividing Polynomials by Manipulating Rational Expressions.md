## Q1
$$\dfrac{3x^5}{3x^5-1}=\dfrac{3x^5-1+1}{3x^5-1}=1+\dfrac{1}{3x^5-1}$$

## Q3
$$\dfrac{x^2-3}{x^2+8}=\dfrac{x^2-3-8+8}{x^2+8}$$

## Q4
$$\dfrac{7x^2+11}{7x^2-x}=\dfrac{7x^2+11-x+x}{7x^2-x}=1+\dfrac{x+11}{7x^2-x}$$

## Q5
$$\dfrac{6x}{2x-3}=\dfrac{3\left(2x-3\right)+3\left(3\right)}{2x-3}=3+\frac{9}{2x-3}$$

## Q6
$$\dfrac{9x^5}{3x^4+2}=\dfrac{3x\left(3x^4+2-2\right)}{3x^4+2}=\dfrac{3x\left(3x^4+2\right)+3x\left(-2\right)}{3x^4+2}=3x-\frac{6x}{3x^4+2}$$
