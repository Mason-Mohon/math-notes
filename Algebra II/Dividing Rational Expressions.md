To divide one rational expression by another, multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260627220159.png)

$$\displaylines{\frac{\left(\frac{3}{x}\right)}{\left(\frac{5}{y}\right)}\\ \\ \frac{3}{x}\cdot\frac{y}{5}=\frac{3y}{5x}}$$

## Dividing Rational Expressions with Multiple Common Factors
$$\displaylines{\frac{2a^2\left(1+b\right)}{3b}=x,\frac{1+b}{5b^2}=y,x\div y\\ \\ \frac{2a^2\left(1+b\right)}{3b}\div\frac{1+b}{5b^2}\\ \\ \frac{2a^2\left(1+b\right)}{3b}\cdot\frac{5b^2}{1+b}=\frac{10a^2b}{3}}$$

## Question 5

## Question 7
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{x^2+3x} {3y^2}\div\dfrac{x+3} {21y^3}\\ \\ \dfrac{x^2+3x} {3y^2}\cdot\dfrac{21y^3}{x+3}=\frac{x\left(x+3\right)}{3y^2}\cdot\frac{21y^3}{x+3}\\ \\ =7xy}$$

## Question 8
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{8n} {2n - 6}\div\dfrac{4n} {10n - 30}=\frac{8n}{2n-6}\cdot\frac{10n-30}{4n}=\frac{8n\cdot10\left(n-3\right)}{4n\cdot2\left(n-3\right)}\\ \\ =\frac{2\cdot10}{2}=10}$$
