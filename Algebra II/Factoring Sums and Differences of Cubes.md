Algebra II Math Academy Polynomials

a^3 + b^3 is sum of cubes

a^3 - b^3 is difference of cubes

![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260701130429.png)
SOAP: Same, Opposite, Always Positive on order of signs

Ex:
$$\displaylines{x^3-8=x^3-2^3\\ \\ =\left(x-2\right)\left(x^2+2x+4\right)}$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{8x^3-64w^3=8\left(x^3-\left(2w\right)^3\right)\\ a=x,b=2w\\ =8(x-2w)\left(x^2+2xw+4w^2\right)}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{z^3-216,a=z,b=6\\ \\ =\left(z-6\right)\left(z^2+6z+36\right)}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{u^3+v^3\\ \\ =\left(u+v\right)\left(u^2-uv+v^2\right)}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{250t^4+128t=2t\left(125t^3+64\right)\\ a=5t,b=4\\ \\ =2t\left(5t+4\right)\left(25t^2-20t+16\right)}$$

## Q5

## Q6
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{p^3}{27}+\dfrac{q^9}{64}\\ a=\frac{p}{3},b=\frac{q^3}{4}\\ \\ =\left(\dfrac{p}{3}+\dfrac{q^3}{4}\right)\left(\dfrac{p^2}{9}-\dfrac{pq^3}{12}+\dfrac{q^6}{16}\right)}$$
