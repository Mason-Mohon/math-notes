## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=2\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{4}+90^{\circ}\right)\\ \\ y=2\cos X\\ \\ X=\frac{x}{4}+90\degree\\ \\ X=\frac{\pi}{2}+n\cdot180\degree\\ \\ 90\degree+n\cdot180\degree=\frac{x}{4}+90\degree\\ \\ x=n\cdot180\degree\cdot4}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{y=\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{3}-15^{\circ}\right)\\ \\ \frac{x}{3}-15\degree=n\cdot180\degree\\ \\ x=n\cdot540\degree+45\degree}$$
