## Q1
$$\lim_{x\to\frac12}\bigg(\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2\arccos(x)\bigg)=\frac{\pi}{3}+2\arccos\left(\frac12\right)=\frac{3\pi}{3}=\pi$$

## Q2
$$\lim_{x\to0}\big(2x\arcsin(x)\big)=0$$

## Q3
$$\lim_{x\to-\infty}(\pi-\arctan(3x+1))=\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{3\pi}{2}$$

## Q4
$$\lim_{x\to\infty}8\arcsin\left(\dfrac{x+1}{2}\right)=\text{undefined}$$

## Q5
$$\lim_{x\to3}\sin\left(\dfrac32\arctan\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt3}\right)\right)=\sin\frac{\pi}{2}=0$$

## Q6
$$\lim_{x\to\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}\tan\left(\dfrac34\arcsin\big(x\big)\right)=\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=1$$

## Q7
$$\lim_{x\to\infty}\sin\!\left(\arctan(x-3)\right)=\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=1$$
