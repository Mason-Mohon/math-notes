## Example: Linear Numerator and No Common Factors
Solve f(x)=0
$$\displaylines{f(x)={\frac{2x+1}{x^{2}+x-6}}\\ \\ =\frac{2x+1}{\left(x+3\right)\left(x-2\right)}\\ \\ 2x+1=0\\ \\ 2x=-1\\ \\ x=-\frac12}$$

## Finding roots of rational functions with quadratic numerators and no common factors
$$\displaylines{f\left(x\right)=\frac{x^2-4x}{x^2+5x+6}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=\frac{x\left(x-4\right)}{\left(x+2\right)\left(x+3\right)},x=0,x=4}$$
## Question 3
$$\displaylines{f(v)=\frac{v^{2}-v-6}{v-4}\\ \\ f\left(v\right)=\frac{\left(v-3\right)\left(v+2\right)}{v-4},v=3,v=-2}$$
## Question 4

## Question 6
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-5x+6}{x^2-4x+4}\\ \\ f(x)=\dfrac{\left(x-2\right)\left(x-3\right)}{\left(x-2\right)^{}\left(x-2\right)}=\frac{\left(x-3\right)}{\left(x-2\right)},x=3}$$

## Question 7

## Question 8
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^3+x^2-16x-16}{2x+8}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=\frac{x^2\left(x+1\right)-16\left(x+1\right)}{2\left(x+4\right)}=\frac{\left(x+1\right)\left(x-4\right)}{2},x=-1,x=4}$$
## Question 9
$$\displaylines{f(m)=\dfrac{m^3+4m^2-12m}{2m^2+5m-7}\\ \\ f(m)=\dfrac{m^3+4m^2-12m}{2m^2+5m-7}=\frac{m\left(m^2+4m-12\right)}{\left(m-1\right)\left(2m+7\right)}=\frac{m\left(m-2\right)\left(m+6\right)}{\left(m-1\right)\left(2m+7\right)},m=0,2,-6}$$
