## Q1
$$\displaylines{\sqrt{3z^2+6z+3},z\ge-1\\ \\ \sqrt3\cdot\sqrt{z^2+2z+1}=\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt{\left(z+1\right)^2}=\sqrt3\left(z+1\right)}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\sqrt{4y^2+40y+100},y\ge-5\\ \\ 2\sqrt{y^2+10y+25}=2(y+5)}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\sqrt{5t^2+20t+20},t\le-2\\ \\ \sqrt5\cdot\sqrt{t^2+4t+4}=\sqrt5\left(-t-2\right)}$$

## Q6
$$\sqrt{64t^4+80t^2+25}=\sqrt{\left(8t^2+5\right)^2}$$

## Q7
$$\sqrt{9r^4s^6-90r^5s^5}=3r^2s^2\sqrt{s^2-10rs}$$

## Q8
$$\sqrt{\dfrac{h^2+10h+25}{(5-h)^2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\left(h+5\right)^2}{(5-h)^2}}=\left|\dfrac{h+5}{5-h}\right|$$

## Q9
$$\sqrt{\dfrac{p^2-22p+121}{p^2+18p+81}}=\,\frac{\sqrt{\left(p-11\right)^2}}{\sqrt{\left(p+9\right)^2}}=\left|\frac{p-11}{p+9}\right|$$
