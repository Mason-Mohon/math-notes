## Q1
$$\displaylines{w^3+5w^2+w+5=0\\ \\ w^2\left(w+5\right)+1\left(w+5\right),w=-5}$$
## Q2
$$\displaylines{b^3-2b^2-6b+12=0\\ \\ 0=b^2\left(b-2\right)-6\left(b-2\right)=\left(b-\sqrt6\right)\left(b+\sqrt6\right)\left(b-2\right),b=-2,-\sqrt6,\sqrt6}$$
## Q3
$$\displaylines{10h^3-4h^2-15h+6=0\\ \\ 2h^2\left(5h-2\right)-3\left(5h-2\right),h=\frac25,\pm\sqrt{\frac32}}$$
## Q4
$$\displaylines{4y^3+6y^2-14y-21=0\\ \\ 2y^2\left(2y+3\right)-7\left(2y+3\right),y=\pm\sqrt{\frac72},-\frac32}$$
