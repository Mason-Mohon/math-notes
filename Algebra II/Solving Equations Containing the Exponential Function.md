## Q1
$$\displaylines{e^{2y}=5\\ \\ 2y=\ln\left(5\right)\\ \\ y=\frac12\ln\left(5\right)}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{e^{x}=\sqrt[3]{5}\\ \\ x=\frac13\ln\left(5\right)}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{e^{x}=\sqrt[3]{10}\\ \\ x=\frac13\ln\left(10\right)=0.768}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{e^{x}=6\\ \\ x=\ln\left(6\right)=1.792}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{e^{4y+3}=\sqrt[3]{2^7}\\ \\ 4y+3=\frac73\ln\left(2\right)\\ \\ y=\frac14\cdot\frac73\ln\left(2\right)-\frac34=-0.346}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{e^{-p/2}+1=8\\ \\ -\frac{p}{2}=\ln\left(7\right)\\ \\ p=-2\ln\left(7\right)=-3.892}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{e^{-3x}-2=5\\ \\ x=-\frac13\ln\left(7\right)=-0.649}$$
