## Q1
$$\displaylines{10^{x+3}=5^{1-2x}\\ \\ \ln10^{x+3}=\ln5^{1-2x}\\ \\ \left(x+3\right)\ln10=\left(1-2x\right)\ln5\\ \\ x\ln10+2x\ln5=\ln5-3\ln10\\ \\ x=\frac{\ln5-3\ln10}{\ln10+2\ln5}=-0.960}$$

## Q2

## Q3
$$\displaylines{e^{x-3}=5^{x+1}\\ \\ x-3=x\ln5+\ln5\\ \\ x\left(1-\ln5\right)=\ln5+3\\ \\ x=\dfrac{\ln5+3}{1-\ln5}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{e^{2x-1}=3\cdot8^{x}\\ \\ 2x-1=x\ln8+\ln3\\ \\ x\left(2-\ln8\right)=\ln3+1\\ \\ x=\frac{\ln3+1}{2-\ln8}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{2^{5x-1}=7^{3-x}\\ \\ \left(5x-1\right)\ln2=\left(3-x\right)\ln7\\ \\ 5x\ln2-\ln2=3\ln7-x\ln7\\ \\ 5x\ln2+x\ln7=3\ln7+\ln2\\ \\ x=\frac{3\ln7+\ln2}{5\ln2+\ln7}}$$


## Q6

## Q1
$$\displaylines{e^{x+2}=2^{x+1}\\ \\ x+2=\left(x+1\right)\ln2\\ \\ x+2=x\ln2+\ln2\\ \\ x-x\ln2=\ln2-2\\ \\ x\left(1-\ln2\right)=\ln2-2}$$
