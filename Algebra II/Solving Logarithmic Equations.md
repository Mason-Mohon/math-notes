Inverse of logarithm is exponentiation.

$$\displaylines{\log_3x=4,x=?\\ \\ 3^{\left(\log_3x\right)}=3^4\\ \\ 3^{\left(\log_3x\right)}=x,x=81}$$


## Solving Logarithmic Equations With Linear Arguments

$$\displaylines{\log_7\left(2x+1\right)=1\\ \\ 7^{\left(\log_7\left(2x+1\right)\right)}=7^1\\ \\ 2x+1=7,x=3}$$

## Rearranging the equation
$$\displaylines{2\log_5\left(x-3\right)+1=5\\ \\ \log_5\left(x-3\right)=\frac42=2\\ \\ 5^{\left(\log_5\left(x-3\right)\right)}=5^2\\ \\ x=5^3+3=28}$$
