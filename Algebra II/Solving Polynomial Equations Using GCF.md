## Q1
$$\displaylines{p(y)=10y^3+5y^2-5y\\ \\ 0=5y\left(2y^2+y-1\right)\\ \\ 0=5y\left(2y-1\right)\left(y+1\right),y=0,\frac12,-1}$$
## Q2

## Q3

## Q4
$$\displaylines{20t^3+5t^2=12t-3t^2\\ \\ 0=20t^3+8t^2-12t=4t\left(5t^2+2t-4\right)=4t\left(5t-3\right)\left(t+1\right),t=0,-1,\frac35}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{4p^2+21p=3p^2+2p^3\\ \\ 0=2p^3-p^2-21p=p\left(2p^2-p-21\right)=p\left(2p-7\right)\left(p+3\right)}$$

## Q6

## Q7

## Q8
$$\displaylines{10y^3-y=2y^3+y\\ \\ 0=8y^3-2y=2y\left(4y^2-1\right)=2y\left(2y-1\right)\left(2y+1\right),y=0,\frac12,-\frac12}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{6y^5+10y^4+10y^3=-2y^4+4y^3\\ \\ 0=6y^5+12y^4+6y^3=6y^3\left(y^2+2y+1\right)=6y^3\left(y+1\right)^2,y=-1,0}$$

## Q10
