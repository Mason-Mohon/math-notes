General formula:
$$\displaylines{A=\frac12\cdot\left\lbrack f\left(a\right)+2\left(f\left(x_1\right)+f\left(x_2\right)+\cdots+f\left(x_{n-1}\right)\right)+f\left(b\right)\right\rbrack\cdot\Delta x\\ \\ \Delta x=\frac{b-a}{n}}$$
Average of left and right Riemann Sums

## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=\dfrac{108}{(x-2)^2},a=5,b=11,n=2\\ \\ \Delta x=\frac{11-5}{2}=3\\ \\ A=\frac12\cdot\left\lbrack12+2\left(\frac{108}{36}\right)+\frac{108}{121}\right\rbrack\cdot3=29}$$
