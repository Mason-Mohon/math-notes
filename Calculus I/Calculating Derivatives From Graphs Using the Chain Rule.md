## Q1
$$\displaylines{h(x)=(f(x))^4,\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=4\left(f\left(x\right)\right)^3\cdot f^{\prime}\left(x\right)\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(4\right)=4\cdot27\cdot1}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{h(x)=\left({f(x)}\right)^7,h^{\prime}\left(3\right)=?\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=7\left(f\left(x\right)\right)^6\cdot f^{\prime}\left(x\right)\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(3\right)=7\cdot1\cdot\frac23}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{h(x)=e^{{f(x)}},h^{\prime}\left(4\right)=?\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=f^{\prime}\left(x\right)e^{f\left(x\right)}\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(4\right)=-2e^3}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{h(x)=\ln\left({f(x)}\right)\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{f^{\prime}\left(x\right)}{f\left(x\right)}\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(3\right)=\frac{-3}{1}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{h(x)=\ln\left({f(x)}\right)+\sqrt{f(x)}\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{f^{\prime}\left(x\right)}{f\left(x\right)}+\frac{f^{\prime}\left(x\right)}{2\sqrt{f\left(x\right)}}\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(3\right)=\frac{\frac32}{4}+\frac{\frac32}{4}=\frac34}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{h(x)=\dfrac{1}{f(x)}+2^{f(x)}\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{f^{\prime}\left(x\right)}{\left(f\left(x\right)\right)^2}+f^{\prime}\left(x\right)2^{f\left(x\right)}\ln2\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(-3\right)=\frac21-4\ln2}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{h(x)=f(g(x)),h^{\prime}\left(2\right)=?\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=f^{\prime}\left(g\left(x\right)\right)\cdot g^{\prime}\left(x\right)\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(2\right)=f^{\prime}\left(1\right)\cdot-1=-1}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{h(x)=f(g(x)),h^{\prime}\left(2\right)=?\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(x\right)=f^{\prime}\left(g\left(x\right)\right)\cdot g^{\prime}\left(x\right)\\ \\ h^{\prime}\left(2\right)=f^{\prime}\left(3\right)\cdot2=1}$$
