## Q1
$$\lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{1+\cos2x}{12\cos^2x}=\frac{1+2\cos^2x-1}{12\cos^2x}=\frac16$$

## Q2
$$\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sin{x}-\sin{2x}}{1-\cos{x}}=\frac{2\sin x-2\sin x\cos x}{1-\cos x}=2\sin x=0$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\lim_{x\to-\frac{\pi}{4}}\dfrac{2+2\cos(4x)}{\cos^2(2x)}=\frac{2+2\left(1-2\sin^2\left(2x\right)\right)}{\cos^2\left(2x\right)}=\\ \\ \frac{4\left(1-\sin^2\left(2x\right)\right)}{\cos^2\left(2x\right)}=4}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\lim_{x\to-\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\cos{x}}=\frac{\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\left(\cos^2\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)-\sin^2\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\right)}=\\ \\ \frac{\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)}{\left(\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\right)\left(\cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)-\sin\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{\frac{\sqrt2}{2}+\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}}$$

## Q5
$$\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos^2{x}}{5\sin^2{x}}=\frac15$$

## Q6
$$\lim_{x\to-\frac{\pi}{4}}\dfrac{3(\cos{x}+\sin{x})}{2\sin^2{x}-1}=-\frac{3\left(\cos x+\sin x\right)}{\cos2x}=-\frac{3}{\cos x-\sin x}=-\frac{3}{\sqrt2}$$


## Q7
$$\lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac{4\cot^2x}{\csc x-1}=\frac{4\left(\csc^2x-1\right)}{\csc x-1}=4\csc x+4=8$$

## Q8
$$\lim_{x\to-\frac{\pi}{4}}\dfrac{\sec^2{x}+2\tan{x}}{\tan{x}+1}=\frac{1+\tan^2x+2\tan x}{\tan x+1}=\tan x+1$$
