## Q5
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260903113226.png)
$$\displaylines{x^{\prime}=-2,y=400,z=625,y^{\prime}=?\\ \\ \sin\theta=\frac{400}{625}=\frac{16}{25}\\ \\ \cos\theta=\frac{x}{625}\\ \\ -\theta^{\prime}\sin\theta=\frac{x^{\prime}}{625}\\ \\ -\frac{16}{25}\theta^{\prime}=-\frac{2}{625}=\frac{1}{200}}$$

## Q6
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260903113941.png)
$$\displaylines{y^{\prime}=-5,z=60,\theta=45\degree\\ \\ \sin\theta=\frac{y}{60}\\ \\ \theta^{\prime}\cos\theta=\frac{y^{\prime}}{60}\\ \\ \theta^{\prime}\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}=-\frac{5}{60}\\ \\ 0^{\prime}=-\frac{1}{12}\cdot\frac{2}{\sqrt2}=-\frac{1}{6\sqrt2}=\frac{\sqrt2}{12}}$$
