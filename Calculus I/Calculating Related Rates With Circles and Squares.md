## Q1
$$\displaylines{\frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=\frac23\cdot\frac{dD}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ A=\pi r^2\\ \\ \frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=2\pi r\cdot\frac{dr}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ 2\pi r=\frac23\\ \\ r=\frac{2}{3\pi}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=4\cdot\frac{dC}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ C=2\pi r,\frac{dC}{\mathrm{d} t}=2\pi\cdot\frac{dr}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ \frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=2\pi r\cdot\frac{dr}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ 2\pi r=4\cdot2\pi\\ \\ r=4}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\frac{dr}{\mathrm{d} t}=3,r=\frac{5}{2\pi}\\ \\ \frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=2\pi r\cdot\frac{dr}{\mathrm{d} t}\\ \\ \frac{dA}{\mathrm{d} t}=2\pi\cdot\frac{5}{2\pi}\cdot3=15}$$

## Q4
