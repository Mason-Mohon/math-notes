## Q6
$$\displaylines{s(t)=-e^{t}\ln{t},\quad t>0,\left|v\left(1\right)\right|=?\\ \\ v\left(t\right)-e^{t}\ln t-\frac{e^{t}}{t}\\ \\ v\left(1\right)=-e\cdot0-\frac{e}{1}=-e\\ \\ =e}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{s(t)=3e^{-t}+3\ln t-3t,\quad t>0.t=3\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=-3e^{-t}+\frac{3}{t}-3\\ \\ v\left(3\right)=-3e^{-3}-2=-2.15\\ \\ =2.15}$$

## Q8
