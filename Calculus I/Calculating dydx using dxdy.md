## Q1
$$\displaylines{x=y-3y^4\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=1-12y^3\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\dfrac{1}{1-12y^3}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{x=\dfrac{8}{y^3}+7y^5\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=-\frac{24}{y^4}+35y^4\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{y^4}{35y^8-24}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{x=5y^2-\dfrac{4}{y},\left(1,1\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=10y+\frac{4}{y^2}\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{y^2}{10y^3+4}\\ \\ \dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}\Bigg\vert_{(x,y)=(1,1)}=\frac{1}{14}}$$
## Q4
$$\displaylines{x=y^3-6y+4,\left(0,2\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=3y^2-6\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{3y^2-6}\\ \\ \dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}\Bigg\vert_{(x,y)=(0,2)}=\frac{1}{12-6}=\frac16}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{x=6\sqrt{y}-2y^2,\left(4,1\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=\frac{3}{\sqrt{y}}-4y\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{\sqrt{y}}{3-4y\sqrt{y}}\\ \\ \dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}\Bigg\vert_{(x,y)=(4,1)}=-1\\ \\ y-1=-x+4\\ \\ y=-x+5}$$
## Q6
$$\displaylines{x=3y-y^2,\left(0,3\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=3-2y\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{3-2y}\\ \\ m=-\frac13}$$
## Q7
$$\displaylines{x=y^2-3y+5,\left(3,2\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD y}=2y-3\\ \\ m=1\\ \\ y=x-1}$$
## Q8
