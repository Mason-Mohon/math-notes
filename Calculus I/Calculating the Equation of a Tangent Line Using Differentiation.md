## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=x^2+5x\\ \\ y^{\prime}=2x+5,m=3\\ \\ y+4=3\left(x+1\right)\\ \\ y=3x-1}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{y=4x+\dfrac{3}{x^2},\left(1,7\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=4-\frac{6}{x^3},m=-2\\ \\ y-7=-2\left(x-1\right)\\ \\ y=-2x+9}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{y=4x^3-9x-1,\left(1,-6\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=12x^2-9,m=3\\ \\ y=3x-9}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{x}+\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{x}},\left(4,5\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}-\frac{3}{x^{\frac32}},m=\frac12-\frac38=\frac18\\ \\ y=\frac18\left(x-4\right)+5\\ \\ 0=\frac18\left(x-4\right)+5\\ \\ -40=x-4\\ \\ x=-36}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{y=6\sqrt{x}+5x-13,\left(1,-2\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=\frac{3}{\sqrt{x}}+5,m=8\\ \\ y=8x-10\\ \\ x=\frac54}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{y=-2\sqrt{x},\left(4,-4\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}},m=-\frac12\\ \\ y+4=-\frac12\left(x-4\right)\\ \\ -8=x-4\\ \\ x=-4}$$

##  Q7
$$\displaylines{y=8x^2-5,\left(1,3\right)\text{ y axis intersection}\\ \\ y^{\prime}=16x,m=16\\ \\ y-3=16\left(0-1\right)\\ \\ y=-13}$$
