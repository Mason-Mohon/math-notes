## Q1
$$\displaylines{\lim\limits_{x\to-3}\dfrac{\sqrt{2x+22}-4}{x+3}=\dfrac{\sqrt{2x+22}-4}{x+3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{2x+22}+4}{\sqrt{2x+22}+4}=\frac{2x+6}{\left(x+3\right)\left(\sqrt{2x+22}+4\right)}\\ \\ =\frac{2}{\sqrt{2x+22}+4}=\frac14}$$

## Q2
$$\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{5-\sqrt{5x}}{5-x}=\dfrac{5-\sqrt{5x}}{5-x}\cdot\frac{5+\sqrt{5x}}{5+\sqrt{5x}}=\frac{5}{5+\sqrt{5x}}=\frac12$$

## Q3

## Q4
$$\lim\limits_{x\to36}\dfrac{x-36}{\sqrt{x}-6}=\dfrac{x-36}{\sqrt{x}-6}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{x}+6}{\sqrt{x}+6}=\sqrt{x}+6=12$$
