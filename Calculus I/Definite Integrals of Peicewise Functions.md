## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\begin{cases}-\dfrac32x-1, & x<2,\\[5 pt] x-4, & x\geq2.\end{cases}\\ \\ \int_{-2}^5f\left(x\right)\differentialD x=\left\lbrack\frac{x^2}{2}-4x\right\rbrack_2^5+\left\lbrack-\frac34x^2-x\right\rbrack_{-2}^2\\ \\ =\left\lbrack\left(\frac{25}{2}-\frac{40}{2}\right)-\left(2-8\right)\right\rbrack+\left\lbrack\left(-3-2\right)-\left(-3+2\right)\right\rbrack\\ \\ =-\frac{15}{2}+\frac{12}{2}+\left(-4\right)=-4-\frac32=-\frac{11}{2}}$$

## Q2

## Q3

## Q4

## Q5
