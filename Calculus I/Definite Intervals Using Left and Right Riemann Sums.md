## Q7
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/4}^{b}f(x)\,\textrm{d}x=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{l=0}^{n-1}\left[\sec^2\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi l}{2n}\right)\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{2n}\right]}\\ \\ \frac{\pi}{2n}=\frac{b+\frac{\pi}{4}}{n}\\ \\ \frac{\pi}{2}=b+\frac{\pi}{4},b=\frac{\pi}{4}\\ \\ x_{l}=-\frac{\pi}{4}+\frac{l\pi}{2n}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=\sec^2\left(x\right)}$$
