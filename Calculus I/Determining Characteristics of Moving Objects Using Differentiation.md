## Q4
$$\displaylines{s(t)=\dfrac{1}{t}+\ln{t},\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=-\frac{1}{t^2}+\frac{1}{t}<0\\ \\ \frac{t-1}{t^2}<0}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{s(t)=(t-3)e^{t},\quad t\geq0.\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=e^{t}+e^{t}\left(t-3\right)\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=2e^{t}+e^{t}\left(t-3\right)\\ \\ 0=2+t-3\\ \\ t=1\\ \\ v\left(1\right)=e-2e=-e}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{s(t)=4te^{-t},\quad t\geq0.\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=4e^{-t}-4te^{-t}\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=4te^{-t}-8e^{-t}\\ \\ 0=4t-8,t=22\\ \\ v\left(2\right)=\frac{4}{e^2}-\frac{8}{e^2}=-\frac{4}{e^2}}$$
