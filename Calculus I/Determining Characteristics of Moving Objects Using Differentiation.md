## Q5
$$\displaylines{s(t)=(t-3)e^{t},\quad t\geq0.\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=e^{t}+e^{t}\left(t-3\right)\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=2e^{t}+e^{t}\left(t-3\right)\\ \\ 0=2+t-3\\ \\ t=1\\ \\ v\left(1\right)=e-2e=-e}$$
