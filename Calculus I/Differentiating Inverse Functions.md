## Q1
$$\displaylines{g(x)=4x^3+2x+6.\\ \\ x=4g^3+2g+6\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{dg}=12g^2+2\\ \\ \left(g^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\dfrac{1}{12(g^{-1}(x))^2+2}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{g(x)=-x^2+\dfrac{2}{x}\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{dg}=-2g-\frac{2}{g^2}=-\frac{2\left(g^3+1\right)}{g^2}\\ \\ \left(g^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\dfrac{\left(g^{-1}(x)\right)^2}{2\left(g^{-1}(x)\right)^3+2}}$$
