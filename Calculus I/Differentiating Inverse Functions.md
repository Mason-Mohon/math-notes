## Q1
$$\displaylines{g(x)=4x^3+2x+6.\\ \\ x=4g^3+2g+6\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{dg}=12g^2+2\\ \\ \left(g^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\dfrac{1}{12(g^{-1}(x))^2+2}}$$

## Q2
