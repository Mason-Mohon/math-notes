## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\textrm{arccot}x+\ln(x^2+1)\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{1}{1+x^2}+\frac{2x}{x^2+1}=\frac{2x-1}{x^2+1}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}{x}-\operatorname{\mathrm{arccsc}}{x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\dfrac{2}{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=5\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}(x^2+3),x=2,m=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{10x}{\left|x^2+3\right|\sqrt{\left(x^2+3\right)^2-1}}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(2\right)=\frac{20}{7\sqrt{48}}=\frac{5}{7\sqrt3}=\frac{5\sqrt3}{21}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\mathrm{arccot}(4x)\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{4}{16x^2+1}}$$


## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\operatorname{\mathrm{arccsc}}\left(2^{x}\right),x=\frac12,m=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{2x\ln2}{\left|2^{x}\right|\sqrt{\left(2^{x}\right)^2-1}}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(\frac12\right)=-\frac{\ln2}{\sqrt1}=-\ln2}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{y=\operatorname{\mathrm{arccot}}\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}-1\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=-\frac{3}{4\left(\dfrac{3x}{4}-1\right)^2+4}=-\frac{3}{4\left(\frac{9x^2}{16}-\frac{6x}{4}+1\right)+4}=-\frac{3}{\frac{9x^2}{4}-6x+8}=-\frac{12}{9x^2-24x+32}}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\operatorname{\mathrm{arccot}}\left(e^{x}\right)\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{e^{x}}{e^{2x}+1}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(0\right)=-\frac12}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{f(x)=x^5\cdot\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}{x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=5x^4\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}x+\frac{x^5}{\left|x\right|\sqrt{x^2-1}}}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\operatorname{\mathrm{arccsc}}x\cdot\arctan x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{\arctan x}{\left|x\right|\sqrt{x^2-1}}+\frac{\operatorname{\mathrm{arccsc}}x}{1+x^2}}$$

## Q10
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+5}{\mathrm{arcsec}\,{x}}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{2x}{\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}x}-\frac{x^2+5}{\left|x\right|\left(\operatorname{\mathrm{arcsec}}x\right)^2\sqrt{x^2-1}}}$$
