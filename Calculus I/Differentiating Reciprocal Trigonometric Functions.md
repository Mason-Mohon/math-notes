## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=2\sin x-\csc x+13x^2,f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2\cos x+\csc x\cot x+26x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\sqrt3+2\cdot\left(\sqrt3\right)+\frac{13\pi}{3}=3\sqrt3+\dfrac{13}{3}\pi}$$

## Q6

## Q8
$$\displaylines{y=x^3\sec x\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=3x^2\sec x+x^3\left(\sec x\tan x\right)\\ \\ =x^2\sec x\left(3+x^{}\tan x\right)}$$
## Q9
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\csc x\sec x.\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\csc x\cot x\sec x+\csc x\tan x\sec x\\ \\ =\csc x\sec x\left(\tan x-\cot x\right)}$$
## Q10
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\sec x\csc x,x=\frac{\pi}{4},m=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\sec x\csc x\cot x+\csc x\sec x\tan x\\ \\ m=f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=-\left(\sqrt2\right)\cdot\sqrt2+2=0}$$

## Q11
