## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=7\sin x+7\cos x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=7\cos x-7\sin x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{7\sqrt2}{2}-\frac{7\sqrt2}{2}=0}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=-4\cos x+2\sin x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=4\sin x+2\cos x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=4}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=x+2\tan x+3\sin x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=1+2\sec^2x+3\cos x}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=6x-\tan x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=6-\sec^2x\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=6-\left(\frac{1}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}\right)^2=6-4=2}$$
