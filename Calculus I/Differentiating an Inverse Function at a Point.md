$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(x\right)\right)}$$

## Q1
$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(3\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(3\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(4\right)}=\frac{1}{\frac14}=4$$

## Q2
$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(3\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(3\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{\frac34}=\frac43$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\ln{(8)}\log_2{x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{\ln8}{x\ln2}=\frac{3}{x}\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(\ln8\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(\ln8\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(2\right)}=\frac{1}{\frac32}\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(\ln8\right)=\frac23}$$


## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{3+4x}{2-3x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{8-12x+9+12x}{\left(2-3x\right)^2}=\frac{17}{\left(2-3x\right)^2}\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(-7\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(-7\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(1\right)}=\frac{1}{17}}$$

## Q5
$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(-1\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(-1\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(-2\right)}=\frac16$$

## Q6
$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(2\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(2\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(5\right)}=-\frac15$$

## Q7
$$\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(0\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(0\right)\right)}=-\frac18$$


## Q8
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{x-2}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{2x^2-4x-x^2}{\left(x-2\right)^2}=\frac{x\left(x-4\right)}{\left(x-2\right)^2}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=-1\\ \\ -\left(x-2\right)=x^2\\ \\ 2=x^2+x\\ \\ 2=x\left(x+1\right)\\ \\ \frac{2}{x}=x+1,x=1\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(-1\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(-1\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(1\right)}=\frac{1}{-3}}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x+4}{x-1},\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(2\right)=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{x-1-x-4}{\left(x-1\right)^2}=-\frac{5}{\left(x-1\right)^2}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=2\\ \\ 2x-2=x+4,x=6\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(2\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(2\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(6\right)}=\frac{1}{-\frac15}=-5}$$

## Q10
$$\displaylines{f(x)=x^2\ln(x-2),\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(0\right)=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x\ln\left(x-2\right)+\frac{x^2}{x-2}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=0=x^2\ln\left(x-2\right),\ln\left(x-2\right)=0,x-2=1,x=3\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(0\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(0\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(3\right)}=\frac19}$$

## Q11
$$\displaylines{f(x)=-3+5e^{x+1},\left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(2\right)=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=5\exponentialE^{x+1}\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=2,1=e^{x+1},x=-1\\ \\ \left(f^{-1}\right)^{\prime}\left(2\right)=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(f^{-1}\left(2\right)\right)}=\frac{1}{f^{\prime}\left(-1\right)}=\frac15}$$
