## Q2
$$\begin{align}\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{9x^4+3}}{2x^2-x} & =\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{x^4\left(9+\dfrac{3}{x^4}\right)}}{2x^2-x}\\[5 pt]  & =\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{x^4}\sqrt{9+\dfrac{3}{x^4}}}{2x^2-x}\\[5 pt]  & =\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{|x^2|\sqrt{9+\dfrac{3}{x^4}}}{2x^2-x}\\  & \\  & =\frac32\end{align}$$

## Q1
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{x\to-\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{25x^2-1}}{10x^2+2x}}=}\frac{x\sqrt{25-\frac{1}{x^2}}}{x\left(10x+2\right)}=0$$

## Q2
$$\lim_{x\to-\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{3x^4+7x^3}}{5x-2}=\frac{\left|x^2\right|\cdot\sqrt{3+\frac{7}{x}}}{5x-2}=\frac{x\cdot\sqrt{3+\frac{7}{x}}}{5-\frac{2}{x}}=\frac{\sqrt3}{5}\lim_{x\to-\infty}x$$

## Q3
$${\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{3x^2-x^5}{\sqrt{3x^8+4x}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{3x^2-x^5}{x^4\sqrt{3+\frac{4}{x^7}}}}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{x^4\cdot\left(\frac{3}{x^2}-x\right)}{x^4\sqrt{3+\frac{4}{x^7}}}=-\frac{x}{\sqrt3}$$

## Q4
$${\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-\infty}\dfrac{12x^5+7}{\sqrt{16x^{10}+5x^9}}=}\frac{x^5\left(12+\frac{7}{x^5}\right)}{x^5\sqrt{16+\frac{5}{x}}}=3$$
