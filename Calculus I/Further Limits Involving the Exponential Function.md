## Q3
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{5}{x-3}\right)^{3x-9}}}\\ \\ n=\frac{5}{x-3},\frac{1}{n}=\frac{x-3}{5}\\ \\ 15\cdot\left(\frac{x-3}{5}\right)=3x-9\\ \\ e^{15}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{6x}\right)^{2x}}}\\ \\ n=\frac{1}{6x},\frac{1}{n}=6x\\ \\ e^{\frac13}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\lim_{z\to0}\left(1-\tan{z}\right)^{2/\tan{z}}}=}\lim_{z\to0}\left(1+\left(-\tan z\right)\right)^{\frac{2}{\tan z}}\\ \\ n=-\tan z,\frac{1}{n}=-\frac{1}{\tan z}\\ \\ =\frac{1}{e^2}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\dfrac{x+2}{x+1}\right)^{2x+2}}\\ \\ \dfrac{x+2}{x+1}=\frac{x+1+1}{x+1}=1+\frac{1}{x+1},n=\frac{1}{x+1},\frac{1}{n}=x+1\\ \\ =e^2}$$
