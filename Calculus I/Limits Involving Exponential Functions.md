$$e=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^{n}$$

## Q1
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{2n}}=}e^2$$

## Q2
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{-20n}}=}\frac{1}{e^{20}}$$

## Q3
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{-n\theta}}=}e^{-\theta}$$

## Q4
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^{3n/x}}=}e^{\frac{3}{x}}$$

## Another Definition
$$e^{x}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{x}{n}\right)^{n}$$
## Q5
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{y}{n}\right)^{-4n}}=}\frac{1}{e^{4y}}$$

## Q6
$${\displaystyle{\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\dfrac{x^2}{n}\right)^{n/x}}=}e^{x}$$
