## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{-x-1} & \textrm{ for }\quad\quad\quad x\leq-5\\[5 pt] \,x+3 & \textrm{ for }\quad-5<x<1.\end{cases}\\ \\ \lim_{x\to-5^{-}}f\left(x\right)=\sqrt{5-1}=2}$$
