## Q3
$$\lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\dfrac13\csc\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac13\cdot\frac{1}{\sec\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}=\frac13\cdot\frac{2}{\sqrt2}=\frac{2}{3\sqrt2}\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=\frac{\sqrt2}{3}$$

## Q5
