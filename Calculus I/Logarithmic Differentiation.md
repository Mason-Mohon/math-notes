## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=2x^{x}\\ \\ y^{\prime}=2x^{x}\cdot g\left(x\right),g\left(x\right)=?\\ \\ \ln y=\ln2x^{x}\\ \\ \ln y=\ln2+x\ln x\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=\ln x+1=g\left(x\right)}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{y=ex^{-2x^2}\\ \\ \ln y=1+\ln x^{-2x^2}=1-2x^2\ln x\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=-4x\ln x-2x\\ \\ y^{\prime}=ex^{-2x^2}\cdot x\left(-4\ln x-2\right)=ex^{1-2x^2}\left(-4\ln x-2\right)}$$


## Q3
$$\displaylines{y=3x^{7x-2x^2}\\ \\ \ln y=\ln3+\left(7x-2x^2\right)\ln x\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=\left(7-4x\right)\ln x+7-2x\\ \\ y^{\prime}=3x^{7x-2x^2}\left(7-4x\right)\ln x+7-2x}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{y=7x^{3x+2}\\ \\ \ln y=\ln7+\left(3x+2\right)\ln x\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=3\ln x+3+\frac{2}{x}\\ \\ y^{\prime}=7x^{3x+1}\cdot\left(3\ln x+3+\frac{2}{x}\right)}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{y=\left(\dfrac{1}{2x}\right)^{x}\\ \\ \ln y=x\ln\left(\frac{1}{2x}\right)\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=\ln\left(\frac{1}{2x}\right)+x\cdot-\frac{1}{2x^2}\cdot2x\\ \\ y^{\prime}=2x^{-x}\left(\ln\left(\frac{1}{2x}\right)-1\right)}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{y=(x^2)^{x}\\ \\ \ln y=x\ln x^2\\ \\ \frac{y^{\prime}}{y}=\ln x^2+x\cdot\frac{1}{x^2}\cdot2x=\ln x^2+2\\ \\ y^{\prime}=\left(x^2\right)^{x}\left(\ln x^2+2\right)}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{y=(x^2)^{2x-1},x=1,m=?\\ \\ \ln y=\left(2x-1\right)\ln\left(x^2\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}=(x^2)^{2x-1}\cdot\left(2\ln x^2+\left(2x-1\right)\cdot\frac{1}{x^2}\cdot2x\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}\left(1\right)=1\cdot\left(0+1\cdot2\right)=2}$$
