## Q1
$$\displaylines{m=8,t=4,v(t)=4\sqrt{t}-5\ln(t-3)\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{t}}-\frac{5}{t-3}\\ \\ a\left(4\right)=1-5=-4\\ \\ F=4\cdot8=32N}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{v(t)=2t-\dfrac13t^3,t=3,m=6\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=2-t^2\\ \\ a\left(3\right)=-7\\ \\ F=6\cdot7=42N}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{s(t)=t^2+4\ln t,t=2,m=2\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=2t+\frac{4}{t}\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=2-\frac{4}{t^2}\\ \\ a\left(2\right)=1\\ \\ F=2N}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{s(t)=\tan^{-1}{t},m=\frac12\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=-\csc^2t\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=2\csc^2t\cot t\\ \\ F=\csc^2t\cot t}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{s(t)=-\ln{\left|\cos{t}\right|},m=2\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=\frac{\sin t}{\cos t}=\tan t\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=\sec^2t\\ \\ F=2\sec^2t}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{s(t)=e^{2t},m=\frac14,t=\ln\sqrt2\\ \\ v\left(t\right)=2e^{2t}\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=4e^{2t}\\ \\ a\left(\ln\sqrt2\right)=4e^{2\ln\sqrt2}=4e^{\ln2}=8\\ \\ F=2N}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{v(t)=t^3-3t^2-24t+8,m=5,F=0\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=3t^2-6t-24\\ \\ 0=t^2-2t-8=\left(t-4\right)\left(t+2\right)\\ \\ t=4}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{s(t)=\dfrac{1}{12}t^4+\dfrac16t^3-3t^2+6t+12\\ \\ m=2,F=0\\ \\ a\left(t\right)=t^2+t-6=\left(t+3\right)\left(t-2\right)\\ \\ t=2}$$
