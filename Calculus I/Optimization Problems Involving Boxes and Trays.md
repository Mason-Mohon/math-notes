## Q1
$$\displaylines{S=125,z=\frac13x\\ \\ V=xyz=\frac13x^2y\\ \\ S=2xy+2yz+2xz\\ \\ 125=2xy+\frac23xy+\frac23x^2=\frac83xy+\frac23x^2\\ \\ -\frac83xy=\frac23x^2-125,y=\left(\frac23x^2-125\right)\cdot-\frac{3}{8x}\\ \\ y=\frac{375}{8x}-\frac14x\\ \\ V=\frac13x^2\left(\frac{375}{8x}-\frac14x\right)=\frac{128}{8}x-\frac{1}{12}x^3\\ \\ V^{\prime}=\frac{125}{8}-\frac14x^2=0\\ \\ x^2=\frac{125}{2},x=\frac{5\sqrt5}{\sqrt2}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{S=726,z=x\\ \\ 726=4xy+2x^2,-4xy=2x^2-726\\ \\ y=\frac{363}{2x}-\frac12x\\ \\ V=x^2y=\frac{363}{2}x-\frac12x^3\\ \\ V^{\prime}=\frac{363}{2}-\frac32x^2=0\\ \\ x^2=\frac{363}{3}=121,x=11}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{z=2x,V=\frac83\\ \\ V=xyz=2x^2y=\frac83,y=\frac{3}{4x^2}\\ \\ S=6xy+4x^2=\frac{8}{x^{}}+4x^2\\ \\ S^{\prime}=-\frac{8}{x^2}+8x=0,x=1\\ \\ S\left(1\right)=12}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{V=243,z=2x\\ \\ V=2x^2y=243,y=\frac{243}{2x^2}\\ \\ S=2xy+4yx+4x^2=\frac{243}{x}+\frac{486}{x}+4x^2=\frac{729}{x}+4x^2\\ \\ S^{\prime}=-\frac{729}{x^2}+8x=0,729=8x^3,x^3=\frac{729}{8},x=\frac92\\ \\ S\left(\frac92\right)=162+81=243}$$

## Q7
