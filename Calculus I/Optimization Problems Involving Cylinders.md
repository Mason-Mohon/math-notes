## Q1
$$\displaylines{V=8\pi=\pi r^2h,h=\frac{8}{r^2}\\ \\ S=2\pi rh+\pi r^2=\frac{16\pi}{r}+\pi r^2\\ \\ S^{\prime}=-\frac{16\pi}{r^2}+2\pi r,r^3=8,r=2}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{S=2\pi rh+\pi r^2,V=216\pi=\pi r^2h,h=\frac{216}{r^2}\\ \\ S=\frac{432\pi}{r}+\pi r^2\\ \\ S^{\prime}=-\frac{432\pi}{r^2}+2\pi r,432\pi=2\pi r^3\\ \\ r^3=216,r=6\\ \\ S\left(6\right)=108\pi}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{S=2\pi rh+2\pi r^2=6\pi\\ \\ h=\frac{6\pi-2\pi r^2}{2\pi r}=\frac{3}{r}-r\\ \\ V=\pi r^2h=3\pi r-\pi r^3\\ \\ V^{\prime}=3\pi-3\pi r^2=0,r^2=1,r=1}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{S=2\pi rh+2\pi r^2=\frac23\pi\\ \\ h=\frac23\pi\cdot\frac{1}{2\pi r}-r=\frac{1}{3r}-r\\ \\ V=\pi r^2\left(\frac{1}{3r}-r\right)=\frac{\pi r}{3}-\pi r^3\\ \\ V^{\prime}=\frac{\pi}{3}-3\pi r^2=0,3\pi r^2=\frac{\pi}{3},r^2=\frac19,r=\frac13}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{V=\frac{125}{3}\pi=\pi r^2h,h=\frac{125}{3r^2}\\ \\ C=15\pi rh+2.50\pi r^2=\frac{625\pi}{r}+2.50\pi r^2\\ \\ C^{\prime}=-\frac{625\pi}{r^2}+5\pi r,r^3=125,r=5}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{V=\frac{27}{4}\pi,h=\frac{27}{4r^2}\\ \\ C=12\pi rh+1.5\pi r^2=\frac{81\pi}{r}+1.5\pi r^2\\ \\ C^{\prime}=-\frac{81\pi}{r^2}+3\pi r,r^3=27,r=3}$$
