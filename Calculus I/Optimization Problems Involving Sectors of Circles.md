$$\displaylines{P=200=2x+2r+\pi r+\pi x=\left(2+\pi\right)\left(x+r\right),r=\frac{200}{2+\pi}-x\\ \\ A=4x\left(\frac{200}{2+\pi}-x\right)+\frac{\pi x^2}{2}+\frac{\pi\left(\frac{200}{2+\pi}-x\right)^2}{2}\\ \\ =\frac{800x}{2+\pi}-4x^2+\frac{\pi x^2}{2}+\frac{\pi}{2}\left(\frac{200}{2+\pi}-x\right)^2\\ \\ A^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{800}{2+\pi}-8x+\pi x-\frac{200\pi}{2+\pi}+\pi x=0\\ \\ \frac{100\left(8-2\pi\right)}{2+\pi}=x\left(8-2\pi\right)\\ \\ x=\frac{100}{2+\pi}}$$
