## Q3
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260819113234.png)
$$\displaylines{r=\frac13\\ \\ b^2=\left(\frac23\right)^2-l^2\\ \\ b=\sqrt{\frac49-l^2}\\ \\ P\left(l\right)=\frac23+l+\sqrt{\frac49-l^2}\\ \\ P^{\prime}\left(l\right)=1-\frac{l}{\sqrt{\frac49-l^2}}=0\\ \\ \sqrt{\frac49-l^2}=l\\ \\ \frac49-l^2=4^2,2l^2=\frac49,l=\frac{\sqrt2}{3}\\ \\ P\left(\frac{\sqrt2}{3}\right)=\frac23+\frac{\sqrt2}{3}+\sqrt{\frac49-\left(\frac{\sqrt2}{3}\right)^2}=\frac{2+2\sqrt2}{3}}$$

## Q4
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260819114004.png)
$$\displaylines{r=2\sqrt2\\ \\ b^2=8-l^2\\ \\ P\left(l\right)=4\sqrt2+l+\sqrt{32-l^2}\\ \\ P^{\prime}\left(l\right)=1-\frac{l}{\sqrt{32-l^2}}=0\\ \\ l=\sqrt{32-l^2},2l^2=32,l=4\\ \\ P\left(2\right)=4\sqrt2+4+4=4\left(2+\sqrt2\right)}$$

## Q5

$$\displaylines{r=3\sqrt2,h=6\sqrt2\\ \\ b=\sqrt{72-l^2}\\ \\ P\left(l\right)=6\sqrt2+l+\sqrt{72-l^2}\\ \\ P^{\prime}\left(l\right)=1-\frac{l}{\sqrt{72-l^2}}=0\\ \\ 72=2l^2,l=6\\ \\ P\left(6\right)=6\sqrt2+6+6=6\left(2+\sqrt2\right)}$$

## Q6
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260819114553.png)
$$\displaylines{r=\frac{\sqrt2}{2},h=\sqrt2\\ \\ P\left(l\right)=\sqrt2+l+\sqrt{2-l^2}\\ \\ P^{\prime}\left(l\right)=1-\frac{l}{\sqrt{2-l^2}}\\ \\ 2-l^2=l^2,l=1\\ \\ P\left(1\right)=\sqrt2+1+1=2+\sqrt2}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{r=3.5,h=7\\ \\ b^2=49-l^2\\ \\ A\left(l\right)=\frac12l\sqrt{49-l^2}\\ \\ A^{\prime}\left(l\right)=\frac12\sqrt{49-l^2}-\frac{l^2}{2\sqrt{49-l^2}}=0\\ \\ l^2=49-l^2,l^2=\frac{49}{2},l=\frac{7\sqrt2}{2}\\ \\ A\left(\frac{7\sqrt2}{2}\right)=\frac12\left(\frac{7\sqrt2}{2}\right)\sqrt{49-\left(\frac{49}{2}\right)}=\frac{49}{4}}$$

## Q8
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260819120218.png)
$$\displaylines{r=\sqrt6,h=2\sqrt6\\ \\ A\left(l\right)=\frac12l\sqrt{24-l^2}\\ \\ A^{\prime}\left(l\right)=\frac12\sqrt{24-l^2}+\frac12\left(l\right)\left(-\frac{2l}{2\sqrt{24-l^2}}\right)\\ \\ 24-l^2=l^2,l=\sqrt{12}\\ \\ A\left(\sqrt{12}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{12}}{2}\cdot\sqrt{12}=6}$$
