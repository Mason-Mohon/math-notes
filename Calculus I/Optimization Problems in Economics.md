## Q1
$$\displaylines{C(x)=\dfrac13x^3-12x^2+128x+30,5<x<20\\ \\ C^{\prime}\left(x\right)=x^2-24x+128=0\\ \\ C^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\left(x-16\right)\left(x-8\right),x=8,16\\ \\ C^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=2x-24=x-12\\ \\ C^{\doubleprime}\left(8\right)=-4<0\\ \\ C^{\doubleprime}\left(16\right)=4>0}$$

Second derivative is positive at 16, meaning it is minimum, meaning it optimizes cost.
