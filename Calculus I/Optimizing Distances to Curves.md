## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=e^{x-1}\\ \\ S\left(x,y\right)=x^2+y^2\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2+e^{2x-2}\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x+2e^{2x-2}=0}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{y=\log_2x\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2+\left(\log_2x\right)^2\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x+\frac{2\log_2x}{x\ln2}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{2x},P\left(2,0\right)\\ \\ S\left(x,y\right)=\left(x-2\right)^2+y^2\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2-2x+4\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x-2=0,x=1}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{4x+5}+2,P\left(3,2\right)\\ \\ S\left(x,y\right)=\left(x-3\right)^2+\left(y-2\right)^2\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2-6x+9+4x+5=x^2-2x+14\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x-2,x=1}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{x}}+3,\left(0,3\right)\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2+\frac{1}{x}\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2x-\frac{1}{x^2},\frac12=x^3,x=\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac13}\\ \\ S\left(\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac13}\right)=\left(\frac12\right)^{\frac23}+2^{\frac13}=\frac{3}{2^{\frac23}}\\ \\ d=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt[3]{2}}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{x^2+1},P\left(1,0\right)\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2-2x+1+x^2+1=2x^2-2x+2\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=4x-2,x=\frac12\\ \\ d=\left(S\left(\frac12\right)\right)^{\frac12}=\frac12-1+2=\frac32}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{y=\sqrt{x^2+1}+2,P\left(2,2\right)\\ \\ S\left(x\right)=x^2-4x+4+x^2+1=2x^2-4x+5\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(x\right)=4x-4,x=1\\ \\ S\left(1\right)=3,d=\sqrt3}$$
