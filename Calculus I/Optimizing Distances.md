Create S(x) for squared distance function:
$$S=\left(x_2-x_1\right)^2+\left(y_2-y_1\right)^2$$

Calculate first derivative and set equal to zero to find stationary points.

Use second derivative test to confirm minimums
## Q1
$$\displaylines{(-3a+5,\,2a),(-1,\,7),\text{minimize the distance}\\ \\ S\left(a\right)=\left(3a-6\right)^2+\left(7-2a\right)^2=9a^2-36a+36+49-28a+4a^2\\ \\ S\left(a\right)=13a^2-64a+85\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(a\right)=26a-64=0,a=\frac{64}{26}=\frac{32}{13}\\ }$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{(a,\,-2a),(4,\,-3).\\ \\ S\left(a\right)=\left(4-a\right)^2+\left(2a-3\right)^2=16-8a+a^2+4a^2-12a+9=5a^2-20a+25\\ \\ S^{\prime}\left(a\right)=10a-20=0,a=2}$$

## Q3

## Q4
