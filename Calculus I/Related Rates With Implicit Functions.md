## Q1
$$\displaylines{e^{x}+y\ln y=2.\dfrac{\textrm{d}x}{\textrm{d}t}=3,\left(\ln2,1\right)\\ \\ e^{x}\cdot\frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD t}+\frac{y}{y}\cdot\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}+\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}\ln y=0\\ \\ 2\cdot3+\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=0\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=-6}$$

## Q2

## Q3
$$\displaylines{y^3+6y^2+12y=-x,\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=2,x=0\\ \\ y=0^{}\\ \\ 3y^2y^{\prime}+12yy^{\prime}+12y^{\prime}=-x^{\prime}\\ \\ 24=-x^{\prime}\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD x}{\differentialD t}=-24}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{y^3-\ln(x^2-3)=1,\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=\frac13,x=2\\ \\ y=1\\ \\ 3y^2y^{\prime}-\frac{2x^{\prime}}{x^2-3}=0\\ \\ x^{\prime}=\frac14}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{\ln(x^3)-y^3=-8,\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=\frac{1}{12},x=1\\ \\ 0-y^3=-8,y=2\\ \\ \frac{3x^2x^{\prime}}{x^3}-3y^2y^{\prime}=0\\ \\ 3x^{\prime}-1\\ \\ x^{\prime}=\frac13}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{2e^{y}-e^{x-3}=1,\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=2,x=3\\ \\ 2e^{y}-1=1\\ \\ e^{y}=1,y=0\\ \\ 2e^{y}y^{\prime}-e^{x-3}x^{\prime}=0\\ \\ 4-x^{\prime}=0}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{x^2+y^2=\dfrac{17}{4},\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=-2,x=\frac12\\ \\ y=2\\ \\ 2x\cdot x^{\prime}+2y\cdot y^{\prime}=0\\ \\ x^{\prime}=8}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{(x-2)^2+y^2=25,y=3,\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=2\\ \\ x=6\\ \\ 2\left(x-2\right)x^{\prime}+2yy^{\prime}=0\\ \\ 8x^{\prime}+12=0\\ \\ x^{\prime}=-\frac32}$$
