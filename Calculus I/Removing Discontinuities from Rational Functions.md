## Q1
$$\displaylines{g(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2+x-2}{x^2-1}, & x\neq1\\[3 pt] b, & x=1\end{cases}\\ \\ b=\lim_{x\to1}g\left(x\right)=\lim_{x\to1}\dfrac{x^2+x-2}{x^2-1}=\frac{\left(x+2\right)\left(x-1\right)}{\left(x-1\right)\left(x+1\right)}=\frac32}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{g(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2+4x+3}{x^2+3x+2}, & x\neq-1\\[3 pt] b, & x=-1\end{cases}\\ \\ b=\lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{x^2+4x+3}{x^2+3x+2}=\frac{\left(x+1\right)\left(x+3\right)}{\left(x+1\right)\left(x+2\right)}=2}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{g(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2-16}{x^2+2x-8}, & x<-4\\[3 pt] \sqrt{kx^2}, & x\geq-4\end{cases}\\ \\ \lim_{x\to-4^{-}}\dfrac{x^2-16}{x^2+2x-8}=\frac{\left(x-4\right)\left(x+4\right)}{\left(x+4\right)\left(x-2\right)}=\frac43\\ \\ \lim_{x\to-4^{+}}\sqrt{kx^2}=4\sqrt{k}\\ \\ \frac43=4\sqrt{k},k=\frac19}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{g(x)=\begin{cases}8x+k, & x\leq2\\[3 pt] \dfrac{x^3+2x^2+(k^2-1)x-1}{x-1}, & x>2\end{cases}\\ \\ \lim_{x\to2^{-}}8x+k=16+k\\ \\ \lim_{x\to2^{+}}\dfrac{x^3+2x^2+(k^2-1)x-1}{x-1}=\frac{8+8+2k^2-3}{1}\\ \\ 16+k=13+2k^2\\ \\ 3=k\left(2k-1\right)\\ \\ 0=2k^2-k-3=\left(2k-3\right)\left(k+1\right)\\ \\ k=\frac32,-1}$$
