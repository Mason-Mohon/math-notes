## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\begin{cases}-5, & x\le-1\\[1 ex] 2ax+3b, & -1<x\le2\\[1 ex] 4, & x>2\end{cases}\\ \\ -5=-2a+3b,4=4a+3b\\ \\ a=1-\frac{3b}{4}\\ \\ -5=-2+\frac{3b}{2}+3b\\ \\ -3=\frac{9b}{2}\\ \\ b=-\frac23,a=\frac32}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\begin{cases}3x^2+1, & x\leq1\\[1 ex] a^2x^2+ax+2, & x>1\end{cases}\\ \\ 4=a^2+a+2\\ \\ 0=a^2+a-2=\left(a+2\right)\left(a-1\right)}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{a}}{a-x}, & 0\leq x<a\\[1 ex] -\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{6}, & x\geq a\end{cases}\\ \\ \dfrac{\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{a}}{a-x}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{a}}=\frac{-1}{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{a}}=-\frac{\sqrt{x}}{6}\\ \\ \frac{1}{2\sqrt{a}}=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{6},a=3}$$

## Q6
