## Q2

## Q3
$$\cos{\theta}+\sin{\theta}\cos^2{\theta}+\sin^3{\theta}=\cos\theta+\sin\theta\left(\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta\right)=\cos{\theta}+\sin{\theta}$$

## Q4

## Q5
$$\dfrac{\sin^3{x}+\sin{x}\cos^2{x}+1}{\cos{x}}=\frac{\sin x+1}{\cos x}=\tan x+\sec x$$

## Q6
$$\dfrac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin\theta\cos^3\theta+\cos\theta\sin^3\theta}=\dfrac{\cos^2\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta\left(\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta\right)}=\cot\theta$$

## Q7
