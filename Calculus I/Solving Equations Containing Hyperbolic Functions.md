$$\displaylines{3\cosh{x}=5\\ \\ 3\left(\frac12\left(e^{x}+e^{-x}\right)\right)=5\\ \\ \frac32e^{2x}-5e^{x}+\frac32=0\\ \\ 3e^{2x}-10e^{x}+3=0\\ \\ \left(3u-1\right)\left(u-3\right)=0\\ \\ x=\ln\left(\frac13\right),\ln3}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{17\tanh{x}=15\\ \\ 17\left(\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{e^{x}+e^{-x}}\right)=15\\ \\ 17e^{x}-17e^{-x}=15e^{x}+15e^{-x}\\ \\ 17e^{2x}-17-15e^{2x}-15=0=2e^{2x}-32=u^2-16=\left(u-4\right)\left(u+4\right)\\ \\ u=\pm4\\ \\ e^{x}=4,x=\ln4}$$

## Q4

## Q5
$$\displaylines{3\cosh{x}-5\sinh{x}=2\\ \\ \frac32e^{x}+\frac32e^{-x}-\frac52e^{x}+\frac52e^{-x}=2\\ \\ \frac32e^{2x}+\frac32-\frac52e^{2x}+\frac52=2e^{x}\\ \\ 4-e^{2x}=2e^{x}\\ \\ 0=e^{2x}+2e^{x}-4\\ \\ e^{x}=\sqrt5-1\\ \\ x=\ln\left(\sqrt5-1\right)}$$

## Q6
