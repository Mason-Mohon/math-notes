## Q1
$$\displaylines{A=xy,P=2x+2y=6\\ \\ y=3-x\\ \\ A=3x-x^2\\ \\ A^{\prime}=3-2x=0,x=\frac32\\ \\ A^{\doubleprime}=-2>0\text{ its a maximum}\\ \\ P\left(\frac32\right)=3+2y=6,y=\frac32\\ \\ \frac94}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{A=xy,P=2x+2y=15\\ \\ y=\frac{15}{2}-x\\ \\ A=\frac{15}{2}x-x^2\\ \\ A^{\prime}=\frac{15}{2}-2x=0,x=\frac{15}{4}\\ \\ 2\left(\frac{15}{4}\right)+2y=\frac{30}{2},y=\frac{15}{4}\\ \\ A=14.06\,\mathrm{m}^2}$$

## Q3
