## Q4
$$\lim_{\theta\to0}\:\theta\cot^2{\theta}\sin{\theta}=\theta\cdot\left(\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\right)^2\cdot\sin\theta=1\cdot\cos^2\theta=1$$

## Q5
$$\lim_{\theta\to0}\dfrac{\tan\theta\sec\theta}{\theta}=\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\right)\cdot\frac{1}{\theta}\cdot\frac{1}{\cos\theta}=1\cdot\frac{1}{\cos^2\theta}=1$$
