## Q3
$$\displaylines{y=\log(4x+6),\left(1,1\right)\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{4}{\left(4x+6\right)\ln10}\\ \\ m=\frac{2}{5\ln10}\\ \\ y=\frac{2x}{5\ln10}-\frac{2}{5\ln10}+1}$$
