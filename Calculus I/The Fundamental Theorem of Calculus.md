## Q1
$${\displaystyle\int_{-1}^1x^8\,\textrm{d}x=}\frac{x^9}{9}\Bigg|_{-1}^1=\frac19+\frac19=\frac29$$

## Q2
$${\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{-1}x^5\,\textrm{d}x=}\frac{x^6}{6}\Bigg|_{-2}^{-1}=\frac16-\frac{64}{6}=-\frac{63}{6}=-\frac{21}{2}$$

## Q3

## Q4
