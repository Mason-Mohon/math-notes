## Q1
$$\displaylines{F(x)={\displaystyle\int_1^{x}8e^{t-1}\,\textrm{d}t,}F(1+\ln2)=?\\ \\ F\left(x\right)=\left\lbrack8e^{t-1}\right\rbrack_{_1}^{x}\\ \\ F(1+\ln2)=\left\lbrack8e^{t-1}\right\rbrack_{_1}^{1+\ln2}=8e^{\ln2}-8e^0=16-8=8}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{F(x)={\displaystyle\int_{\pi/4}^{x}2\sin t\,\textrm{d}t,F\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=?}\\ \\ F\left(x\right)=\left\lbrack-2\cos t\right\rbrack_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{x}\\ \\ F\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=-2\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)+2\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=0+2\cdot\frac{\sqrt2}{2}=\sqrt2}$$
