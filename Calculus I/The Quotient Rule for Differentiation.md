## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{6x}{1-3x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{\left(6-18x\right)-\left(-18x\right)}{9x^2-6x+1}=\frac{6}{9x^2-6x+1}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{10}{3-x^2}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\dfrac{20x}{\left(3-x^2\right)^2}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{\ln{x}}{x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{\frac{x}{x}-\ln x}{x^2}=\frac{1-\ln x}{x^2}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x}{\cos x}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\dfrac{\cos x+x\sin x}{\cos^2x}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{y=\dfrac{2e^{x}}{x+1},x=1,m=?\\ \\ \frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD x}=\frac{2e^{x}\left(x+1\right)-2e^{x}}{\left(x+1\right)^2}\\ \\ m=\frac{4e^{}-2e}{4}=\frac{e}{2}}$$
