In essence, the second derivative test uses the function's concavity at the stationary point to decide whether it's a local maximum or a local minimum.

## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=-2x^3+12x+9,f^{\prime}(-\sqrt2)=0\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-6x^2+12\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=-12x\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(-\sqrt2\right)=12\sqrt2>0}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=x^4-8x^3+24x^2-32x+12,f^{\prime}\left(2\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=4x^3-24x^2+48x\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=12x^2-48x+48\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(2\right)=48-96+48=0}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^5}{5}-\dfrac{x^4}{2}+\dfrac{1}{10},f^{\prime}\left(0\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=x^4-2x^3\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=4x^3-6x^2}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=4x^5+5x^4-10x^2-20x+30,f^{\prime}\left(-1\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=20x^{^4}+20x^3-20x-20\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=80x^3+60x^2-20}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=2x^5-5x^4+10\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=10x^4-20x^3=x^3\left(10x-20\right),x=0,2\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=40x^3-60x^2\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(0\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(2\right)=80>0}$$
