# The Second Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
2026-08-12

Second theorem:
$$\displaylines{F\left(x\right)=\int_{a}^{x}f\left(t\right)\differentialD t\\ \\ F^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{d}{\differentialD x}\int_{a}^{x}f\left(t\right)\differentialD t=f\left(x\right)}$$

 Taking the derivative "cancels out" the integral and leaves us with the integrand. This happens because the derivative and the integral are _opposite_ operations.
