## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=(x^2-2x-8)e^{x+1}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=e^{x+1}\left(2x-2\right)+(x^2-2x-8)e^{x+1}\\ \\ 2-2x=x^2-2x-8\\ \\ 0=x^2-10=\left(x-\sqrt{10}\right)\left(x+\sqrt{10}\right)}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=(x-1)e^{2x+4}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2(x-1)e^{2x+4}+e^{2x+4}=0\\ \\ -\frac12=\left(x-1\right),x=\frac12}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x-4}{x^2-7}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{\left(x^2-7\right)-2x\left(x+4\right)}{\left(x^2-7\right)^2}=0=-x^2+8x-7=x^2+8x+7=\left(x+1\right)\left(x+7\right)}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x-3}{x^2-5}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{\left(x^2-5\right)-2x^2+6x}{\left(x^2-5\right)^2}=\frac{6x-x^2-5}{\left(x^2-5\right)^2}=0\\ \\ x^2-6x+5=0=\left(x-5\right)\left(x-1\right)}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x-2}{x^2+6}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{x^2+6-2x^2+4x}{\left(x^2+6\right)}=0=x^2-4x-6\\ \\ x=\frac{4\pm\sqrt{16+24}}{2}=2\pm\sqrt{10}}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{f(x)=3\sin{3x}+\cos{3x},x\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{\pi}{3}\right),x_1\cdot x_2=?\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=9\cos3x-3\sin3x=0\\ \\ 3\cos3x=\sin3x\\ \\ 3=\tan3x\\ \\ x=\frac{\arctan3+n\pi}{3}\\ \\ -\pi<3x<\pi\\ \\ x_1=\frac{\arctan3}{3},x_2=\frac{\arctan3-\pi}{3}\\ \\ -0.26}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\sin x+4\cos x,x\in\left(0,2\pi\right)\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\cos x-4\sin x=0\\ \\ \cot x=4\\ \\ x=\arctan\left(\frac14\right)+n\pi\\ \\ x_1=0.24,x_2=3.39}$$
