## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{5x-x^2}{2x-3x^2}\\ \\ =\frac{x\left(5-x\right)}{x\left(2-3x\right)}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x^2+6x+8}\\ \\ =\frac{\left(x-4\right)\left(x+4\right)}{\left(x+4\right)\left(x+2\right)}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{(x-2)(x^3+3x^2+x+3)}{x^2+x-6}\\ \\ f(x)=\dfrac{(x-2)\left(x+3\right)\left(x^2+1\right)}{\left(x-2\right)\left(x+3\right)}}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{1}{4x}-\dfrac{1}{x+5}\\ \\ =\frac{x+5-4x}{4x\left(x+5\right)}}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x^2-4}-\dfrac{1}{x-4}\\ \\ =\frac{x\left(1-x\right)}{\left(x-2\right)\left(x+2\right)\left(x-4\right)}}$$
