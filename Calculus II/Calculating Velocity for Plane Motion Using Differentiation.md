# Calculating Velocity for Plane Motion Using Differentiation

Subject: Calculus II

$$\displaylines{\mathbf{r}=\left\langle x\left(t\right),y\left(t\right)\right\rangle\text{ position}\\ \\ \mathbf{v}=\frac{\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}}{\mathrm{d} t}=\left\langle\frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\mathrm{d} t},\frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} t}\right\rangle=\left\langle u,v\right\rangle\text{ velocity}\\ \\ \left|\mathbf{v}\right|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}=\sqrt{\left(\frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\mathrm{d} t}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} t}\right)^2}\text{       speed}}$$
