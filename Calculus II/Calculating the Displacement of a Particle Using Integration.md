Total displacement is difference in position between t=a and t=b

$$\displaylines{d=x\left(b\right)-x\left(a\right)=\int_{a}^{b}x^{\prime}\left(t\right)\differentialD t\\ \\ d=\int_{a}^{b}v\left(t\right)\differentialD t}$$


## Q1
$$\displaylines{v(t)=9t-t^2,t=\left\lbrack0,6\right\rbrack\\ \\ d=\int_0^6\left(9t-t^2\right)\differentialD t\\ \\ =\left\lbrack\frac92t^2-\frac13t^3\right\rbrack_0^6\\ \\ 182-72=90}$$
