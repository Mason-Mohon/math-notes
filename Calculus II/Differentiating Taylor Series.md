## Q8
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}\sqrt{n}\left(\dfrac{x}{6}\right)^{n}}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac16\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(-1\right)^{n}\sqrt{n^3}\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)^{n-1}\\ \\ L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}\right|=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{\left(-1\right)\left(-1\right)^{n}\sqrt{\left(n+1\right)^3}\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)^{n-1}}{\left(-1\right)^{n}\sqrt{n^3}\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)^{n-1}}\right|\\ \\ =\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)\frac{\sqrt{\left(n+1\right)^3}}{\sqrt{n^3}}\cdot\frac{\frac{1}{n^{\frac32}}}{\frac{1}{n^{\frac32}}}\Rightarrow\frac{x}{6}<1\Rightarrow x<6\\ \\ R=6}$$
