Partial fraction decomposition
## Q1
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{3x+10}{(x+4)(x+5)}=\frac{A}{\left(x+4\right)}+\frac{B}{\left(x+5\right)}\\ \\ 3x+10=A\left(x+5\right)+B\left(x+4\right)\\ \\ x=-5\Rightarrow-5=-B\Rightarrow B=5\\ \\ x=-4\Rightarrow A=-2}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{x+5}{(x-1)(x+1)}=\dfrac{3}{(x-1)}+\dfrac{B}{(x+1)}\\ \\ x+5=3\left(x+1\right)+B\left(x-1\right)\\ \\ x=-1\Rightarrow4=-2B,B=-2}$$
