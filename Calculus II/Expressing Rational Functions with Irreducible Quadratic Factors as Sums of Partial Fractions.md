$$\displaylines{\frac{3x^2-x+1}{\left(x^2+2\right)\left(x-1\right)}=\frac{Ax+B}{x^2+2}+\frac{C}{x-1}\\ \\ 3x^2-x+1=\left(Ax+B\right)\left(x-1\right)+C\left(x^2+2\right)\\ \\ =\left(A+C\right)x^2+\left(B-A\right)x+\left(2C-B\right)\\ \\ A+C=3,B-A=-1,2C=B=1}$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{9x^2-6x+20}{(2x^2+5)x}=\frac{x-B}{2x^2+5}+\frac{4}{x}\\ \\ 9x^2-6x+20=\left(x-B\right)x+4\left(2x^2+5\right)\\ \\ x=1\\ \\ B+23=29,B=6}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{2x^2+3x+5}{(x^2+4)(x+1)}=\frac{Ax+B}{5\left(x^2+4\right)}+\frac{C}{5\left(x+1\right)}\\ \\ 10x^2+15x+25=\left(Ax+B\right)\left(x+1\right)+C\left(x^2+4\right)\\ \\ 10x^2+15x+25=Ax^2+Ax+Bx+B+Cx^2+4C\\ \\ =x^2\left(A+C\right)+x\left(A+B\right)+\left(4C+B\right)\\ \\ 10=A+C,15=A+B,25=4C+B\\ \\ A=10-C\\ \\ B=24-4C\\ \\ A+B=15,C=4}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{7x^2-29x+34}{(x^2-4x+5)(x-3)}=\frac{Ax+B}{(x^2-4x+5)}+\frac{C}{x-3}\\ \\ 7x^2-29x+34=Ax^2-3Ax+Bx-3B+Cx^2-4Cx+5C\\ \\ 7x^2-29x+34=\left(A+C\right)x^2+\left(B-3A-4C\right)x+\left(5C-3B\right)\\ \\ A+C=7,A=7-C\\ \\ B-3A-4C=-29\Rightarrow B=C-8\\ \\ 5C-3B=34\Rightarrow C=5,A=2,B=-3\\ \\ \dfrac{2x-3}{(x^2-4x+5)}+\dfrac{5}{(x-3)}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{3x^2+4x+13}{(x^2+5)(2x-1)}=\frac{Ax+B}{x^2+5}+\frac{C}{2x-1}\\ \\ 3x^2+4x+13=\left(2A+C\right)x^2+\left(2B-A\right)x+\left(5C-B\right)\\ \\ 2A+C=3,2B-A=4,5C-B=13\\ \\ \dfrac{2}{(x^2+5)}+\dfrac{3}{(2x-1)}}$$
