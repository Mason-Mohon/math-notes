# Finding Areas Between Curves that Intersect at More Than Two Points

$$\displaylines{f\left(x\right)=x^3-2x,g\left(x\right)=2x\\ \\ f\left(x\right)=g\left(x\right)\Rightarrow x=0,2,-2\text{ Find intersection points}\\ \\ x\in\left\lbrack-2,0\right\rbrack,A_1=\int_{-2}^0\left(f\left(x\right)-g\left(x\right)\right)\\ \\ \text{Regions are symmetrical}\\ \\ A_1=A_2\\ \\ A=A_1+A_2}$$

# Review

## Q1
$$\displaylines{y=f(x)=2x^3-6x^2,y=g(x)=2x-6\\ \\ 2x^3-6x^2=2x-6\\ \\ x^3-3x^2-x+3=x^2\left(x-3\right)-1\left(x-3\right)\Rightarrow x=-1,1,3\\ \\ A_1=\int_{-1}^1\left(f\left(x\right)-g\left(x\right)\right)\mathrm{d} x=\int_{-1}^1\left(2x^3-6x^2-2x+6\right)\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\left\lbrack\frac12x^4-2x^3-x^2+6x\right\rbrack_{-1}^1=\left(\frac12-2-1+6\right)-\left(\frac12+2-1-6\right)=-4+12=8\\ \\ A_2=8,A=16}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{y=f(x)=x^3-4x^2+x+3,g\left(x\right)=-3\\ \\ 0=x^3-4x^2+x+6\Rightarrow x=-1,2,3\\ \\ A_1=\int_{-1}^2\left(x^3-4x^2+x+6\right)\mathrm{d} x=\left\lbrack\frac{x^4}{4}-\frac{4x^3}{3}+\frac{x^2}{2}+6x\right\rbrack_{-1}^2=\frac{88}{12}+\frac{47}{12}=\frac{135}{12}\\ \\ A_2=\int_2^3\left(-x^3+4x^2-x-6\right)\mathrm{d} x=\left\lbrack-\frac{x^4}{4}+\frac{4x^3}{3}-\frac{x^2}{2}-6x\right\rbrack_2^3=-\frac{81}{12}-\frac{88}{12}=\frac{7}{12}\\ \\ A=\frac{71}{6}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{y=f(x)=x^3+x^2-4x,y=4\\ \\ 0=x^3+x^2-4x-4=x^2\left(x+1\right)-4\left(x+1\right)\Rightarrow x=-2,-1,2\\ \\ A_1=\int_{-2}^{-1}\left(x^3+x^2-4x-4\right)\mathrm{d} x=\left\lbrack\frac{x^4}{4}+\frac{x^3}{3}-2x^2-4x\right\rbrack_{-2}^{-1}=\frac{23}{12}-\frac{16}{12}=\frac{7}{12}\\ \\ A_2=\left\lbrack-\frac{x^4}{4}-\frac{x^3}{3}+2x^2+4x\right\rbrack_{-1}^2=\frac{112}{12}+\frac{23}{12}=\frac{125}{12}\\ \\ A=\frac{71}{6}}$$
