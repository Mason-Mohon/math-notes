$$S_{\infty}=\frac{a_1}{1-r},\left|r\right|<1$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{10+4+\dfrac85+\dfrac{16}{25}+\cdots\\ \\ r=\frac{4}{10}\\ \\ =10\cdot\frac{10}{6}=\frac{50}{3}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{-18-9-\dfrac92-\dfrac94-\cdots.\\ \\ r=\frac12\\ \\ S_{\infty}=-36}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{-7.5+1.5-0.3+0.06+\cdots.\\ \\ r=-\frac15\\ \\ S_{\infty}=-\frac{15}{2}\cdot\frac56=-\frac{75}{12}=-\frac{25}{4}=-6.25}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{6.4+4.8+3.6+2.7+\cdots\\ \\ r=\frac{48}{64}=\frac34\\ \\ S_{\infty}=\frac{64}{10}\cdot4=25.6}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{r^{n-m}=\frac{a_{n}}{a_{m}},a_3=36,a_6=\frac{36}{27}\\ \\ r^{\left(6-3\right)}=\frac{36}{27}\cdot\frac{1}{36}=\frac{1}{27}\\ \\ r=\frac13\\ \\ S_{\infty}=324\cdot\frac32=486}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{a_5=40,a_8=5\\ \\ r^3=\frac{5}{40}=\frac18,r=\frac12\\ \\ S_{\infty}=640\cdot2=1280}$$
