# Harmonic Series and p-Series
2026-11-04
Math Academy
Subject: Calculus II
Topics: Harmonic Series

p-series
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{p}}$$

If p>1, series converges
If p<=1, series diverges

Aka harmonic series, used as an example of a series that diverges even though its terms approach 0.
