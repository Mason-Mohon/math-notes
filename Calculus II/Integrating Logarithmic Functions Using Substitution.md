## Q1
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\int\dfrac{6\ln(x^3)}{x}\,\textrm{d}x}.}\\ \\ u=\ln\left(x^3\right),u^{\prime}=\frac{3x^2}{x^3}=\frac{3}{x}\differentialD x\\ \\ 2\int udu=u^2+C=\ln^2(x^3)+C}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\int\dfrac{\ln x}{2x}\,\textrm{d}x}.}\\ \\ u=\ln x,\frac{du}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{x}\\ \\ =\dfrac14\ln^2x+C}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x\log_2{x}}\,\textrm{d}x}\\ \\ u=\log_2x,\frac{du}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{x\ln2},\ln2du=\frac{1}{x}\differentialD x\\ \\ \ln2\int\frac{1}{u}du=\ln2\ln u+C=\ln{2}\ln|\log_2{x}|+C}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\int\dfrac{3\log_6^2{x}}{x}\,\textrm{d}x}\\ \\ u=\log_6x,\frac{du}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{x\ln6},\ln6du=\frac{1}{x}\differentialD x\\ \\ 3\ln6\int u^2du=\ln6\cdot\log_6^3{x}+C}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\int_3^{81}\dfrac{2}{x\sqrt{\log_3x}}\,\textrm{d}x},u=\log_3x,\frac{du}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{x\ln3}}\\ \\ 2\ln3\int_1^4\frac{1}{\sqrt{u}}du=2\ln3\left\lbrack\frac12\sqrt{u}\right\rbrack_1^4=\ln3\left(2-1\right)=1\cdot\ln3}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle{\int_{e/3}^{e^2/3}\dfrac{5\ln(3x)}{4x}\,\textrm{d}x},u=\ln\left(3x\right),\frac{du}{\differentialD x}=\frac{1}{x}}\\ \\ \frac54\int_1^2udu=\left\lbrack\frac58u^2\right\rbrack_1^2=\frac{20}{8}-\frac58=\frac{15}{8}}$$
