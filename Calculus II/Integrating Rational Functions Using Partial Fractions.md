## Q1

## Q3
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\int_8^{14}\dfrac{8}{x^2-4x-12}\,\textrm{d}x}\\ \\ \dfrac{8}{x^2-4x-12}\,=\dfrac{8}{\left(x-6\right)\left(x+2\right)}\Rightarrow8=A\left(x+2\,\right)+B\left(x-6\right)\\ \\ x=-2\Rightarrow8=-8B,B=-1\\ \\ x=6\Rightarrow A=1\\ \\ \int_8^{14}\frac{1}{x-6}\mathrm{d} x-\int_8^{14}\frac{1}{x+2}\mathrm{d} x=\left\lbrack\ln\left|x-6\right|-\ln\left|x+2\right|\right\rbrack_8^{14}\\ \\ \left(\ln\left|8\right|-\ln\left|16\right|\right)-\left(\ln\left|2\right|-\ln\left|10\right|\right)\\ \\ =\ln\left(\frac12\right)-\ln\left(\frac15\right)=\ln\left(\frac52\right)}$$

## Q4
