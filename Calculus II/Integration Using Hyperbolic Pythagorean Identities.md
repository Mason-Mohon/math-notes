$$\displaylines{\int\left(\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x\right)\mathrm{d} x=\int1\mathrm{d} x=x+C\\ \\ 1+\sinh^2x=\cosh^2x\\ \\ \operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}x=\frac{1}{\cosh x}\\ \\ \int\frac{1}{1+\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{1}{\cosh^2x}\mathrm{d} x=\int\left(\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}^2x\right)\mathrm{d} x=\tanh x+C}$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{\int\frac{1}{\cosh x-1}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{1}{\cosh x-1}\cdot\frac{\cosh x+1}{\cosh x+1}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{\cosh x+1}{\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\int\mathrm{csch}\operatorname{}^2x\left(\cosh x+1\right)\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{\cosh x}{\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x+\int\mathrm{csch}\operatorname{}^2x\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =-\mathrm{csch}x-\coth x+C}$$

## Q2
$$\int\frac{\coth^2x}{\sinh^2x+1}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{1}{\cosh^2x}\cdot\frac{\cosh^2x}{\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x=\int cs\operatorname{\mathrm{ch}}^2x\mathrm{d} x=-\coth x+C$$


$$\displaylines{1-\tanh^2x=\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}^2x\\ \\ 1-\coth^2x=-cs\operatorname{\mathrm{ch}}^2x}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\mathrm{csch}\operatorname{}^2x\left(1-\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}^2x\right)\mathrm{d} x=\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\mathrm{csch}\operatorname{}^2x\tanh^2x\mathrm{d} x=\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}^2x\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\left\lbrack\tanh x\right\rbrack_{\ln2}^{\ln3}=\frac15}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\int_0^{\ln5}\left(\sinh x\tanh^2x+\sinh x\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}^2x\right)\mathrm{d} x=\int_0^{\ln5}\left(\sinh x\right)\mathrm{d} x=\left\lbrack-\cosh\right\rbrack_0^{\ln5}\\ \\ \frac{e^{\ln5}+e^{-\ln5}}{2}-\frac{1+1}{2}=\frac{13}{5}-1=\frac85}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\frac{\operatorname{csch} x + \operatorname{csch}^{3} x}{\coth x}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}x+\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x}\left(\frac{1}{\sinh^3x}\right)\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\operatorname{\mathrm{sech}}x+\frac{1}{\sinh^2x\cosh x}\mathrm{d} x=\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\frac{\sinh^2x+1}{\sinh^2x\cosh x}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{\cosh x}{\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\left\lbrack-\frac{1}{\sinh x}\right\rbrack_{\ln2}^{\ln3}=\frac{7}{12}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{\int\frac{\operatorname{csch}^{3} x + \operatorname{csch} x}{\coth x}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\left(\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\sinh^3x}\right)+\left(\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\sinh^{}x}\right)\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ =\int\frac{1}{\sinh^2x\cosh x}+\frac{1}{\cosh x}\mathrm{d} x=\int\frac{\cosh x}{\sinh^2x}\mathrm{d} x=-\mathrm{csch}x+C}$$

## Q7
$$\int\frac{6}{1+\sin^2\left(3x+2\right)}\mathrm{d} x=6\int\frac{1}{\cosh^2\left(3x+2\right)}\mathrm{d} x=2\tanh\left(3x+2\right)+C$$

## Q8
$$\int\frac{\cosh^3\left(2x-1\right)}{1+\sinh^2\left(2x-1\right)}\mathrm{d} x=\frac12\sinh\left(2x-1\right)+C$$
