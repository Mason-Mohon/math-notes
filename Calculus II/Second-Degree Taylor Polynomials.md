$$P_2\left(x\right)=f\left(a\right)+f^{\prime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)+\frac12f^{\doubleprime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)^2$$
## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{1}{1-2x},x=0,u=\left(1-2x\right),du=-2,f\left(u\right)=u^{-1},f\left(0\right)=1\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=\frac{2}{\left(1-2x\right)^2},f^{\prime}\left(0\right)=2\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=\frac{8}{\left(1-2x\right)^3},f^{\doubleprime}\left(0\right)=8\\ \\ P_2\left(x\right)=1+2x+4x^2}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\sin(2x),x=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=2\cos\left(2x\right),f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=-4\sin\left(2x\right),f^{\doubleprime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=-4\\ \\ P_2\left(x\right)=1-2\left(x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^2}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{1}{(x-1)^2},x=-3\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{2}{\left(x-1\right)^3}\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=\frac{6}{\left(x-1\right)^4},f^{\doubleprime}\left(-3\right)=\frac{6}{256}\\ \\ \frac{3}{256}}$$
