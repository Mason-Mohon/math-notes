# Solving First-Order ODEs Using Separation of Variables

$$\displaylines{\frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x}=e^{x-y}=\frac{e^{x}}{e^{y}}\\ \\ e^{y}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x}=e^{x}\\ \\ \int e^{y}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x}\mathrm{d} x=\int e^{x}dx\\ \\ \int e^{y}\cdot\mathrm{d} y=\int e^{x}dx\\ \\ e^{y}=e^{x}+C\\ \\ y=\ln\left(e^{x}\right)}$$

## Q1
$$\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=\dfrac{3x}{5xy+y}=\frac{3x}{5x+1}\cdot\frac{1}{y}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=\dfrac{2^{x}}{y^3}\\ \\ y^3\cdot\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=2^{x}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=\dfrac{1+y^2}{x^2}=\left(1+y^2\right)\cdot x^{-2}\\ \\ \int\frac{1}{\left(1+y^2\right)}\mathrm{d} y=\int\frac{1}{x^2}\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ \arctan y=-\frac{1}{x}+C}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=e^{x}\cos^2y\\ \\ \int\sec^2y\cdot\mathrm{d} y=\int e^{x}\cdot\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ \tan y=e^{x}+C}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{y^{\prime}=y\sec{x}\tan{x}\\ \\ \int\frac{1}{y}\mathrm{d} y=\int\sec x\tan x\cdot\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ \ln y=\sec x+C\\ \\ y=e^{\sec x}\cdot e^{C}=Ke^{\sec x}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=\dfrac{2x}{3y^2}\\ \\ \int y^2dy=\frac23\int xdx\\ \\ \frac13y^3=\frac13x^2+C\\ \\ y=\sqrt[3]{x^2+C}}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=6x\sqrt[3]{y^2}\\ \\ \int y^{-\frac23}dy=6\int x\cdot\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ 3\sqrt[3]{y}=3x^2+C\\ \\ y=\left(x^2+C\right)^3}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{\sqrt{xy}\,\dfrac{\textrm{d}y}{\textrm{d}x}=1\\ \\ \int\sqrt{y}\mathrm{d} y=\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ \frac23\sqrt{y^3}=2\sqrt{x}+C\\ \\ y=\left(3\sqrt{x}+C\right)^{\frac23}}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{\sqrt{1-x^2}\,y^{\prime}-y=0\\ \\ \frac{\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x}=\frac{y}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\\ \\ \int\frac{1}{y}\mathrm{d} y=\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\mathrm{d} x\\ \\ \ln y=\arcsin x+C\\ \\ y=Ke^{\arcsin x}}$$
