Average value of a function
$$f_{avg}=\frac{1}{b-a}\int_{a}^{b}f\left(x\right)\differentialD x$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{g(x)=(x-3)^2,\left\lbrack2,5\right\rbrack\\ \\ f_{avg}=\frac13\int_2^5\left(x-3\right)^2\differentialD x,u=x-3\\ \\ f=\frac13\int_{-1}^2u^2du,\left\lbrack\frac19u^3\right\rbrack_{-1}^2=\frac89+\frac19=1}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{P(t)=150+10\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi t}{3}\right),\left\lbrack0,6\right\rbrack\\ \\ f=\frac{75}{\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}du+\frac{5}{\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\sin\left(u\right)du\\ \\ =\left\lbrack\frac{75}{\pi}u-\frac{5}{\pi}\cos u\right\rbrack_0^{2\pi}=\left(150-\frac{5}{\pi}\right)+\frac{5}{\pi}=150}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=-15\sqrt{x}+6,\left\lbrack0,c\right\rbrack\\ \\ -4=\frac{1}{c}\int_0^{c}-15\sqrt{x}+6\differentialD x\\ \\ -4=\frac{1}{c}\left(-10c^{\frac32}+6c\right)=-10\sqrt{c}+6,c=1}$$
