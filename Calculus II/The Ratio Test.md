## Q1
$$\displaylines{a_{n}=\dfrac{(-1)^{n+1}}{7^{n}}\\ \\ {\displaystyle L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\dfrac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}\right|}\\ \\ \dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{7\cdot7^{n}}\cdot\frac{7^{n}}{\left(-1\right)^{n}\left(-1\right)}=\left|-\frac17\right|=\frac17}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{a_{n}=\dfrac{6^{n}}{3n!}.\\ \\ {\displaystyle L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\dfrac{6\cdot6^{n}}{3n!\cdot\left(n+1\right)}\cdot\frac{3n!}{6^{n}}\right|}=\left|\frac{6}{n+1}\right|=0}$$

## Q3

## Q4

## Q5
