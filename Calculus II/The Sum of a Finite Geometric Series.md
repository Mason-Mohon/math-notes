$$\displaylines{S_{N}=a_1\frac{\left(1-r^{N}\right)}{1-r}\\ \\ r=\frac{a_2}{a_1}}$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{81+54+36+\cdots\\ \\ r=\frac23\\ \\ S_6=81\cdot\frac{\left(1-\frac23^6\right)}{1-\frac23}=\left(\frac{729-64}{729}\right)\cdot3=81\cdot\frac{665}{729}\cdot3=\frac{665}{3}}$$
