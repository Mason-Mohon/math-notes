# Third-Degree Taylor Polynomials

Second degree, quadratic approximation:
$$P_2\left(x\right)=f\left(a\right)+f^{\prime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)+\frac12f^{\doubleprime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)^2$$

Third degree cubic approximation:
$$P_3\left(x\right)=f\left(A\right)+f^{\prime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)+\frac{1}{2!}f^{\doubleprime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)^2+\frac{1}{3!}f^{\prime\prime\prime}\left(a\right)\left(x-a\right)^3$$

## Q1
$$\displaylines{f(-3)=5,f^{\prime}(-3)=-1,f^{\doubleprime}(-3)=1\\ \\ P_3\left(x\right)=5-(x+3)+\dfrac12(x+3)^2+\dfrac13(x+3)^3}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{f(-2)=4,f^{\prime}(-2)=-3,f^{\doubleprime}(-2)=-8\\ \\ P_3\left(x\right)=4-3\left(x+2\right)-4\left(x+2\right)^2+\frac53\left(x+2\right)^3}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f\left(x\right)=\cos^2{x},x=\frac{\pi}{4}\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-2\cos x\sin x,f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=-1\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=-2\cos^2x+2\sin^2x,f^{\doubleprime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=0\\ \\ f^{\prime\prime\prime}\left(x\right)=8\cos x\sin x,f^{\prime\prime\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=4\\ \\ P_3\left(x\right)=\frac12-\left(x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\frac23\left(x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^3}$$

## Q4

## Q5
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x},x=-2\\ \\ f^{\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{1}{x^2}=-\frac14\\ \\ f^{\doubleprime}\left(x\right)=\frac{2}{x^3}\\ \\ f^{\prime\prime\prime}\left(x\right)=-\frac{6}{x^4}=-\frac38\cdot\frac16=-\frac{1}{16}}$$
