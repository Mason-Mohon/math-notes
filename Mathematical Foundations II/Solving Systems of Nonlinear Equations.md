## Q3
$$\displaylines{\begin{cases}x=y^2+y+5\\[5 pt] x=-3y+1\end{cases}\\ \\ -3y+1=y^2+y+5\\ \\ 0=y^2+4y+4=\left(y+2\right)^2,y=-2,x=7}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\begin{cases}x=y^2+6y+20\\[5 pt] x=4y-3\end{cases}\\ \\ 4y-3=y^2+6y+20\\ \\ 0=y^2+2y+23\\ \\ \mathcal{D}=4-48}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{\begin{cases}x=3y^2-4y\\ 8x-7y^2=5x\end{cases}\\ \\ -3x=-7y^2\\ \\ 3y^2-4y=\frac{7y^2}{3}\\ \\ 2y^2-12y=0=2y-12,y=6\\ \\ x=3\cdot36-24}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{\begin{cases}x=y^2-6y\\[5 pt] 5y^2=2x\end{cases}\\ \\ y^2-6y=\frac52y^2\\ \\ -3y^2-12y=0=y+4,y=4\\ \\ x=40}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{\begin{cases}y=x^2-8x\\[5 pt] 7x^2=3y\end{cases}\\ \\ x^2-8x=\frac73x^2\\ \\ -4x^2-24x=0=x^{}+6,x=-6,y=84}$$

## Q8
