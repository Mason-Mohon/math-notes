## Q1
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{2\left(w-3\right)}{w\left(w-3\right)}+\dfrac{3}{w(w-3)}=\dfrac{w}{w\left(w-3\right)}\\ \\ 2w-6+3=w\\ \\ w=3\text{ no valid solutions}}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{1}{4x}+\dfrac{1}{2x(3x+1)}=\dfrac{1}{3x+1}\\ \\ \dfrac{4x\left(3x+1\right)}{4x}+\dfrac{4x\left(3x+1\right)}{2x(3x+1)}=\dfrac{4x\left(3x+1\right)}{3x+1}\\ \\ =3x+3=4x\\ \\ x=3}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{1}{x-1}=\dfrac{5}{x^2-1}-\dfrac{2}{x+1}\\ \\ \dfrac{1}{x-1}=\dfrac{5}{\left(x+1\right)\left(x-1\right)}-\dfrac{2}{x+1}\\ \\ x+1=5-2x+2\\ \\ 3x=6\\ \\ x=2}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{-\dfrac{1}{r+1}=\dfrac{1}{r-1}-\dfrac{2}{r^2-1}\\ \\ 1-r=r-1\\ \\ r=1}$$

## Q5
