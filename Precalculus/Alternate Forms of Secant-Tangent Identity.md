$$\displaylines{1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta\\ \\ \tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta-1\\ \\ \sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta=1}$$

## Q1
$$1-2\sin^2{x}+\sec^2{x}-2\cos^2{x}=1+\sec^2x-2=\tan^2x$$

## Q2
$$\dfrac{\sec^2\theta-1}{\tan^3\theta}=\frac{1}{\tan\theta}$$

## Q3
$$\dfrac{\sec x-1}{\tan^2x}=\frac{\sec x-1}{\left(\sec x-1\right)\left(\sec x+1\right)}=\frac{1}{\sec x+1}$$

## Q4
$$\tan\theta\,\sec^2\theta-\dfrac{1}{\cot^3\theta}=\tan\theta\left(\sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta\right)=\tan\theta$$

## Q5
$$\sec^4x-2\sec^2x+1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x\left(\sec^2x-2+1\right)=\sec^2x\tan^2x$$

## Q6
$$\sec^2\theta\tan^2\theta-\sec^4\theta+2\sec^2\theta-1=\sec^2\theta\left(\tan^2\theta-\sec^2\theta\right)+2\sec^2\theta-1=\tan^2\theta$$
