# Review

## Q1
$$\displaylines{\frac{x+y}{11.4}=\frac{y}{1.9}\\ \\ 1.9x=9.5y,x=5y\\ \\ x^{\prime}=1,y^{\prime}=0.2}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\frac{x+y}{300}=\frac{y}{120},x^{\prime}=90\\ \\ x=\frac32y\\ \\ y^{\prime}=60}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\frac{x+y}{180}=\frac{y}{66},x^{\prime}=48\\ \\ x=\frac{114}{66}y\\ \\ 48=\frac{114}{66}y^{\prime}=27.8}$$
