## Q1
$$\dfrac{-3-4\textrm{i}}{1-\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{-3-4\textrm{i}}{1-\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{1+i}{1+i}=\frac{-3-3i-4i+4}{2}=\frac{1-7i}{2}$$
## Q2
$$\dfrac{3-4\textrm{i}}{2+\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{3-4\textrm{i}}{2+\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{2-i}{2-i}=\frac{6-3i-8i-4}{5}=\frac25-\frac{11i}{5}$$
## Q3
$$\dfrac{-6+5\textrm{i}}{4+7\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{-6+5\textrm{i}}{4+7\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{4-7i}{4-7i}=\frac{-24+42i+20i+35}{65}=\frac{11}{65}+\frac{62}{65}i$$
## Q4
$$\dfrac{12-19\textrm{i}}{8-11\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{12-19\textrm{i}}{8-11\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{8+11i}{8+11i}=\frac{96+132i-152i+209}{185}=\frac{315}{185}-\frac{20}{185}i=\frac{63}{37}-\frac{4}{37}i$$
## Q5
$$\dfrac{3+2\textrm{i}}{-2+5\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{3+2\textrm{i}}{-2+5\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{-2-5i}{-2-5i}=\frac{-6-15i-4i+10}{4+10i-10i+25}=\frac{4}{29}-\frac{19}{29}i$$
## Q6
$$\dfrac{3\textrm{i}}{-2+5\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{3\textrm{i}}{-2+5\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{-2-5i}{-2-5i}=\frac{15}{29}-\frac{6}{29}i$$
## Q7
$$\dfrac{7\textrm{i}}{-1-4\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{7\textrm{i}}{-1-4\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{-1+4i}{-1+4i}=-\frac{28}{17}-\frac{7i}{17}$$

## Q8
$$\dfrac{5}{-2+3\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{5}{-2+3\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{-2-3i}{-2-3i}=-\frac{10}{13}-\frac{15}{13}i$$

## Q10
$$\dfrac{7-3\textrm{i}}{2\textrm{i}}=\dfrac{7-3\textrm{i}}{2\textrm{i}}\cdot\frac{-2i}{-2i}=-\frac32-\frac{14}{4}i$$
