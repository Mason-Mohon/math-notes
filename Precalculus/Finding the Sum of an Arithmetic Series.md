$$\displaylines{S_{N}=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_{n}\\ \\ S_{N}=\frac{N}{2}\left(a_1+a_{N}\right)}$$


## Q1
$$\displaylines{-6+(-2)+2+6+\cdots+50,n=15\\ \\ d=4\\ \\ a_{n}=4n-10\\ \\ \frac{15}{2}\left(-6+50\right)=22\cdot15}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{(-5)+\left(-\dfrac92\right)+(-4)+\left(-\dfrac72\right)+\cdots+\dfrac{13}{2}\\ \\ d=-\frac92+\frac{10}{2}=\frac12\\ \\ a_{n}=-\frac{10}{2}+\left(n-1\right)\left(\frac12\right)\\ \\ a_{N}=\frac{13}{2}=-\frac{11}{2}+\frac12N\\ \\ N=24\\ \\ S_{N}=12\left(-\frac{10}{2}+\frac{13}{2}\right)=18}$$
