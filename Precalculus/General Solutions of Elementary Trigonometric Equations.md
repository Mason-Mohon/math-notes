## Q1
$$\displaylines{\cos{x}=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\\ \\ x_1=\frac{3\pi}{4}\\ \\ x_2=\pi+\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{5\pi}{4}\\ \\ x=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2n\pi,\quad x=\dfrac{5\pi}{4}+2n\pi}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\cos x=\frac12\\ \\ x_1=\frac{\pi}{3}\\ \\ x_2=2\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{5\pi}{3}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\csc{x}=-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}\\ \\ \sin x=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\\ \\ \theta_1=240\degree\\ \\ \theta_2=300\degree}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{3\csc x=2\sqrt3\\ \\ \sin x=\frac{3}{2\sqrt3}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\\ \\ x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}+2n\pi,\quad x=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}+2n\pi}$$
