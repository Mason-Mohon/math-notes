# Rational Equations With Three Terms
2026-10-29

Subject: Precalculus
Topics: Rational Equations
## Q1
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{2q+5}{q^2}+\dfrac{q+1}{q^2}=\dfrac{4q+3}{q^2}\\ \\ 3q+6=4q+3\\ \\ q=3}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{b+5}{b}+\dfrac{b-7}{b}=6\\ \\ 2b-2=6b\\ \\ b=-\frac12}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{3y-1}{2y}+\dfrac{y-5}{6y}=2\\ \\ 9y-3+y-5=12y\\ \\ y=-4}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{x+3}{x}=\dfrac{x-4}{4x}+\dfrac{1}{x}\\ \\ 4x+12=x\\ \\ x=-4}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{2}{t}=\dfrac{3+t}{5t}+\dfrac{3}{10t}\\ \\ 20=6+2t+3\\ \\ t=\frac{11}{2}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{z+1}{z}+\dfrac{3z-2}{2z}=-\dfrac{5}{6z}\\ \\ 6z+6+9z-6=-5\\ \\ z=-\frac13}$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{3x-1}{x^2}=\dfrac{2}{x}-\dfrac{x-3}{x^2}\\ \\ 3x-1=x+3\\ \\ x=2}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{2}{4z^2}-\dfrac{z+3}{2z^2}=\dfrac{3z-1}{z^2}\\ \\ 2-2z-6=12z-4\\ \\ 0=14z,\text{ no solution}}$$
