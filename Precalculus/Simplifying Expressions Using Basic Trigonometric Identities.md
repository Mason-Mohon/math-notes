## Q8
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\sin{\theta}+\cot\theta}{\tan\theta+\csc{\theta}}\\ \\ =\left(\sin\theta+\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\right)\div\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}+\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\right)=\left(\frac{\sin^2\theta+\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\right)\div\left(\frac{\sin^2\theta+\cos\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}\right)\\ \\ =\cos\theta}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\tan\theta+\sin\theta}{\sin\theta\cot\theta+1}\\ \\ =\left(\frac{\sin\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\cos\theta}\right)\div\left(\cos\theta+1\right)=\frac{\sin\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\cos^2\theta+\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\left(\frac{1+\cos\theta}{\cos\theta+1}\right)=\tan\theta}$$
