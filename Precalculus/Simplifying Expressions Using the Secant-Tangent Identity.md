## Q1
$$4\tan^2\theta-2\sec^2\theta+4=2\sec^2\theta$$

## Q2
$$\left(\tan\theta-1\right)^2+2\tan\theta=\tan^2\theta-2\tan\theta+1+2\tan\theta=\sec^2\theta$$

## Q4
$$\left(\sec x+1\right)^2-2\sec x=\sec^2x+1=2+\tan^2x$$

## Q5
$$\dfrac{\tan{\theta}\cdot\sec{\theta}}{1+\tan^2{\theta}}=\frac{\tan\theta}{\sec\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\cos\theta=\sin\theta$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{{\cos^2x\ \!({\sec^2x}}-2\tan^2x)=\cos^2x\left(1+\tan^2x-2\tan^2x\right)=\cos^2x\left(1-\tan^2x\right)\\ \\ =\cos^2x-\frac{\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}\cdot\cos^2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x=1-2\sin^2x}$$
