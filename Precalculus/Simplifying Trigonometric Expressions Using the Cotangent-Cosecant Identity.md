## Q1
$$\displaylines{\,\sin x\left(1+{\cos x\cdot\cot x\cdot\csc x}\right)\!=\sin x\left(1+\frac{\cos x}{1}\cdot\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}\cdot\frac{1}{\sin x}\right)=\sin x\left(1+\cot^2x\right)\\ \\ =\sin x\left(\csc^2x\right)=\csc x}$$

## Q2
$$\dfrac{\csc^2\theta}{\cot\theta}-\cot{\theta}=\frac{1+\cot^2\theta}{\cot\theta}-\cot\theta=\tan\theta+\cot\theta-\cot\theta=\tan\theta$$

## Q3
$$\dfrac{\cot\theta}{\csc^2{\theta}-1}=\frac{1}{\cot\theta}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{\cos\theta\,\cot\theta}{\csc\theta-1}\cdot\frac{\csc\theta+1}{\csc\theta+1}=\frac{\cos\theta\cot\theta\left(\csc\theta+1\right)}{\cot^2\theta}=\frac{\cos\theta\cdot\frac{1}{\sin\theta}+\cos\theta}{\cot\theta}=\frac{\cot\theta+\cos\theta}{\cot\theta}\\ \\ =1+\cos\theta\cdot\tan\theta=1+\sin\theta}$$

## Q5
$$\cos^2x\csc^2x-\cos^2x\cot^2x+\sin^2x=\cos^2x\left(\csc^2x-\cot^2x\right)+\sin^2x=1$$

## Q6
$$\sec^2x \csc^2x - \sec^2x \cot^2 x.$$

## Q7
$$\dfrac{\cos\theta\cot^2\theta}{\csc\theta-1}-\cot\theta=\cos\theta\left(\csc\theta+1\right)-\cot\theta=\cos\theta$$

## Q8
$$\dfrac{\csc\theta+1}{\cot^2\theta}=\dfrac{1}{\csc\theta-1}$$
