## Q5

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\ln(x+1)-4<\ln(x-1)\\ \\ \left(x+1\right)\cdot e^{-4}<x-1\\ \\ \left(x+1\right)<xe^4-e^4\\ \\ 1+e^4<x\left(e^4-1\right)\\ \\ x>\dfrac{1+e^4}{e^4-1}}$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{\ln(3x)\leq\ln(x-1)+5\\ \\ 3x\le\left(x-1\right)e^5\\ \\ x\geq\dfrac{e^5}{e^5-3}}$$

## Q4
