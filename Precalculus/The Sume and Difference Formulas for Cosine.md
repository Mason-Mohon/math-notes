## Q1
$$\displaylines{\dfrac{17\pi}{12}=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\\ \\ \cos\left(\dfrac{17\pi}{12}\right)=\cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)+\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot-\frac12+\frac{\sqrt6}{4}=\\ \\ \frac{\sqrt2+\sqrt6}{4}}$$
