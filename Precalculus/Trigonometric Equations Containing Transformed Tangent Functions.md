## Q1
$$\displaylines{5\tan\left(x+30^{\circ}\right)-5=0\\ \\ \tan X=1\\ \\ x+30\degree=45\degree+180\degree\cdot n\\ \\ x=15\degree+180\degree\cdot n\\ \\ x_1=15\degree\\ \\ x_2=195\degree}$$

## Q2
$$\displaylines{\tan\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\sqrt3\\ \\ x+\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{3}+n\pi\\ \\ x=n\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}\\ \\ x_1=\frac{5\pi}{6}\\ \\ x_2=\frac{11\pi}{6}}$$


## Q3
$$\displaylines{5\tan\left(2x-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)+5=0\\ \\ 2x-\frac{\pi}{4}=-\frac{\pi}{4}+n\pi\\ \\ x=\frac{n\pi}{2}\\ \\ x_1=0\\ \\ x_2=\frac{\pi}{2}\\ \\ x_3=\pi\\ \\ x_4=\frac{3\pi}{2}}$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{\tan\left(2x-90^{\circ}\right)=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\\ \\ 2x-90\degree=-30\degree+180\degree n\\ \\ x=30\degree+90\degree n}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{\tan\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}\\ \\ \frac{x}{2}=-\frac{\pi}{6}+n\pi\\ \\ x=2n\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}\\ \\ x_1=-\frac{\pi}{3}\\ \\ x_2=\frac{5\pi}{3}}$$

## Q6
$$\displaylines{4\tan\left(2x+30^{\circ}\right)-4\sqrt3=0\\ \\ \tan X=\sqrt3\\ \\ 2x+30\degree=60\degree+180\degree n\\ \\ x=15\degree+90\degree n\\ \\ x_1=15\degree\\ \\ x_2=105\degree}$$
