## Q1
$$f(x)=\dfrac{2}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x+5},x=-5,1$$

## Q2
$$f(x)=\dfrac{5}{(x-4)(x-2)(x+2)},x=4,2,-2$$

## Q3
$$\displaylines{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-x-2}{x^3+7x^2-x-7}\\ \\ \frac{\left(x-2\right)\left(x+1\right)}{x^2\left(x+7\right)-1\left(x+7\right)}=\frac{\left(x-2\right)}{\left(x-1\right)^{}\left(x+7\right)},x=1,-7}$$
