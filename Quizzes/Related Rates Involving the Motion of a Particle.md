$$x(t) = \dfrac{4t}{(t^2+1)^2}, \qquad t \gt 0$$
## Q1
$$\dfrac{\textrm{d}}{\textrm{d}t}\left[(t^2+1)^2\right]=\,4t\left(t^2+1\right)$$

## Q2
$$x^{\prime}(t)=\,\frac{4(t^2+1)^2-16t^2\left(t^2+1\right)}{(t^2+1)^4}=\frac{4(t^2+1)^{}-16t^2}{(t^2+1)^3}=\frac{4-12t^2}{(t^2+1)^3}$$

## Q3
$$x^{\prime}\left(1\right)=\frac{-8}{2^3}=-1$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{x^{\prime}\left(t\right)=0\\ \\ \frac{4-12t^2}{(t^2+1)^3}=0=4-12t^2\\ \\ t^2=\frac13,t=\sqrt{\frac13}=\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}$$

## Q5
$$\frac{\differentialD y}{\differentialD t}=\frac{1-3t^2}{t\left(t^2+1\right)}$$

## Q6
$$y^{\prime}\left(1\right)=-\frac22=-1$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{y^{\doubleprime}\left(t\right)=\frac{-6t^2\left(t^2+1\right)-\left(1-3t^2\right)\left(3t^2+1\right)}{\left(t\left(t^2+1\right)\right)^2}=\frac{3t^4-6t^2-1}{t^2\left(t^2+1\right)^2}\\ \\ y^{\doubleprime}\left(1\right)=\frac{-4}{4}}$$
