$$x(t) = \dfrac{4t}{(t^2+1)^2}, \qquad t \gt 0$$

## Q1
$$\dfrac{\textrm{d}}{\textrm{d}t}\left[(t^2+1)^2\right]=\,4t^3+4t$$

## Q2
$$x^{\prime}\left(t\right)=\frac{4\left(t^2+1\right)^2-16t^2\left(t^2+1\right)}{\left(t^2+1\right)^4}=\frac{4-12t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^3}$$

## Q3
$$x^{\prime}\left(1\right)=\frac{4-12}{8}=-1$$

## Q4
$$\displaylines{x^{\prime}\left(t\right)=0=4-12t^2\\ \\ t^2=\frac13\\ \\ t=\pm\frac{\sqrt3}{3},t>0\\ \\ t=\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}$$

## Q5
$$\displaylines{y=\ln x=\ln\left(\dfrac{4t}{(t^2+1)^2}\right)\\ \\ y^{\prime}\left(t\right)=\frac{4-12t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^3}\cdot\frac{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}{4t}=\frac{4-12t^2}{4t^3+4t}=\frac{1-3t^2}{t^3+t}}$$

## Q6
$$y^{\prime}\left(1\right)=\frac{1-3}{2}=-1$$

## Q7
$$\displaylines{y^{\prime\prime}\left(t\right)=\frac{-6t\left(t^3+t\right)-\left(3t^2+1\right)\left(1-3t^2\right)}{\left(t^3+t\right)^2}\\ \\ y^{\prime\prime}\left(1\right)=\frac{-6\left(2\right)-\left(4\right)\left(-2\right)}{4}=-\frac44=-1}$$

## Q8
$$\displaylines{y^{\prime}(t)=\dfrac{At+B}{t^2+1}+\dfrac{C}{t}\\ \\ 1-3t^2=At^2+Bt+Ct^2+C\\ \\ -3t^2+0t+1=\left(A+C\right)t^2+Bt+C\\ \\ C=1,B=0\\ \\ A+C=-3,A=-4}$$

## Q9
$$\displaylines{{\displaystyle\int\dfrac{-4t}{t^2+1}\,\textrm{d}t}\\ \\ u=t^2+1\Rightarrow\frac{\mathrm{d} u}{\mathrm{d} t}=2t\\ \\ -2\int\frac{1}{u}\mathrm{d} t=-2\ln\left(t^2+1\right)+K}$$

## Q10
$$\displaylines{\int y^{\prime}\left(t\right)\mathrm{d} t=\int\frac{-3t^2+1}{t^3+t}\mathrm{d} t\\ \\ =\ln\left(\frac{t}{(t^2+1)^2}\right)+K}$$
